Around the House

The hot tub is on the back deck. The touch panel has arrows for the water temperature, a button for the jets and a button for the lights.

Use of the hot tub is at your own risk. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. Please follow all posted safety guidelines, and if you have any health concerns or are under medical treatment, consult your doctor before you use it.

The built-in gas grill is on the deck next to the hot tub. Grill use is at your own risk.

  • Step 1: open the left cabinet and turn the propane tank on by turning it counterclockwise.
  • Step 2: hold the leftmost burner knob on for 3 seconds and light it with a lighter. Lighters are in the drawer to the left of the kitchen sink.
  • Step 3: turn on the next burners to the right one at a time. They should light automatically.

Tip: the outdoor seating has cushions, so please cover them on bad-weather days and before you leave.

The fire pit follows Lake Travis Fire + Rescue regulations, so please follow any burn bans. Never leave a fire unattended, and put it out completely before you go inside.

The indoor fireplace is a beautiful focal point in the living room. Use of the fireplace is at your own risk.

The living room has an 84-inch pull-down projector screen with a Roku and an Amazon Fire TV. Movie night is easy:

  • Step 1: lower the projector screen with its wall control and turn the projector on with the power button on the projector remote. The blackout shades help on sunny days.
  • Step 2: use the dial on the projector remote to switch to HDMI 1, which is the Roku player.
  • Step 3: sign in to Roku. When it asks, enter your check-out date so your data is cleared and stays private, then pick your favorite app and sign in.

If the picture does not fit the screen: open the settings gear on the projector home screen, choose Projection Image Setting, then Manual Keystone. Use the arrow keys to move each corner of the picture to the corner of the screen and press OK to switch corners. Press the back button when the green circle looks as round as possible.

The game room has a ping pong table and board games. Before each game, please make sure the table's locking mechanism is fully engaged. A Bluetooth sound system is there for your music; please keep the volume considerate, especially early in the morning and late at night.

The game room has its own space heater and standalone A/C unit, which you can run on their own when you need them.

The full kitchen is ready for group meals, with pans, a blender, a Cuisinart cheese grater, spatulas, tongs and a selection of herbs and spices. There is a coffee machine, a water filter on the kitchen sink, and a wet bar with assorted glassware for mixing drinks.

The dining room table seats the group, and there is a dining table and non-breakable tableware outside for al fresco feasts. Fresh towels and linens are provided, and there are board games for rainy days.

Vaulted ceilings, a stone fireplace and big windows make the sunken living room the heart of the house. The sectional is a regular couch, not a pull-out.

There are four outdoor seating areas to choose from: the sectional lounge on the deck, the outdoor dining table, the screened porch with hammocks, and the balcony off the primary bedroom. The fully fenced backyard has plenty of room for kids and dogs to play.

The washer and dryer are in the laundry room off the kitchen. Press the power button and add detergent: pods or sheets go inside the drum, and powdered detergent goes in the dispenser.

Primary suite: a king bed plus two twin trundle beds, with a balcony overlooking Lakeway and Hurst Creek.

Bedroom 2: a queen bed.

Bedroom 3: a twin-over-full bunk bed. Bunk bed use is at your own risk. Please do not exceed the weight limits, and children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Bedroom 4: a queen bed.

The two queen bedrooms and the bunk room are on the ground level, next to the downstairs bathroom.

There are 2 bathrooms. The primary ensuite has double vanities, a soaking tub and a walk-in shower, and the downstairs bathroom has a full tub and shower. Shampoo, conditioner, body soap, shower gel and a hair dryer are provided.
