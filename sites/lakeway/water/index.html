Top 5 Lake Travis
& Water Spots

Marinas, parks and paddling, with honest winter notes.

The best Lake Travis and water spots near Lakeway are Lakeway Marina for boat and jet ski rentals, Lakeway City Park for free lake access, Mansfield Dam Park for a swim cove and picnics, Bob Wentz Park at Windy Point for windsurfing and paddling, and Austin Paddleboard & Kayak for calm paddling. Check each card for winter closures.

Lake Travis is under a mile away, but the water is cold in winter and lake levels change what you can reach. This list says plainly what is seasonal or closed. Hours and fees come from each operator's or park's own page as of the date below. We take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Lakeway Marina

$$$ 10 min drive Boat and jet ski rentals

The full-service marina on the Lakeway shore of Lake Travis, and the easiest way to get on the water from the house. It rents pontoons, tritoons, runabouts, party barges, jet skis, and paddleboards and kayaks, and sells ethanol-free 93 fuel. Book online ahead for weekends and holidays, since the rental fleet is popular. Parking is on site and the dock office handles check-in, so plan on arriving a little before your rental time.

Per the marina's site: off-season 8 AM to 5 PM daily; in-season Monday 8 AM to 7 PM, Tuesday to Sunday 8 AM to 8 PM. The site does not say which dates count as off-season, so expect the shorter hours in winter and confirm which rentals (jet skis, party barges) run before you plan around them.

 Pro Tip

Book the boat at rentals.lakewaymarina.com before you drive over, and ask at the office what the current lake level means for launching and for the hour you plan to return.

No. 02

Lakeway City Park

Free 10 min walk Free lake access with dogs

A waterfront city park on a cove of Lake Travis, with nearly two miles of trails, a butterfly garden, a wildflower meadow, two playgrounds, sand volleyball and a fenced Bark Park. Swimming, fishing and kayaking happen from designated areas, and there is a life jacket loaner station at the lower picnic area. It is the closest lake access to the house and costs nothing. How much shoreline you get depends on the lake level, and no lifeguards are on duty.

Open daily 6 AM to 10 PM during Daylight Saving Time and 6 AM to 8 PM during Standard Time (city site); in winter, plan on the 8 PM close. Swim only in designated areas; no lifeguards. The city has no winter swim closure, but the water is cold.

The Bark Park is the park's own fenced dog area; other trails are leash-required (confirm current rules with Lakeway Parks and Recreation, 512-314-7530).

 Pro Tip

Walk the Lake Loop first, then let the dog run in the Bark Park and finish with a picnic at the lower level by the water.

No. 03

Mansfield Dam Park

$ 20 min drive Swim cove and picnics

A 71-acre Travis County park below the big views of Lake Travis, with a designated swim cove, a playground, seven covered shelters, barbecue grills, picnic tables and paved multi-use trails. It also has the largest public boat ramp on the lake and an underwater dive park with sunken boats and dam-construction relics. Day-use entry is a few dollars a person and charged in cash only at the gate. Access to the dam itself is not allowed.

Park hours are every day, sunrise to civil twilight, so winter days end around 6 PM; the boat ramp is open 24 hours. Cash only at the entrance. No lifeguard; the county notes zebra mussels along the shoreline.

Pets are allowed but off-leash pets are prohibited, so keep dogs leashed.

 Pro Tip

Bring cash for the gate ($5 an adult, $3 for 62 and older, free for ages 12 and under) and watch for zebra mussels near the shoreline.

No. 04

Bob Wentz Park at Windy Point

$ 30 min drive Windsurfing, paddling, picnics

A 211-acre Travis County park on a breezy point of Lake Travis, known for windsurfing, sailing and scuba. The shoreline has swimming, a sand volleyball court, showers, 18 covered picnic tables and a pavilion with big lake views. The ramp is non-motorized only, so it suits kayaks and paddleboards rather than power boats. On holiday weekends the park fills and closes to new entries for the day, so arrive early.

Open every day 8 AM to 6 PM; closes to new entries 30 minutes before closing. Cash only at the gate. The county's page shows the non-motorized dock closed for high water, and lake level changes what you can launch.

Pets allowed but off-leash pets are prohibited, and no pets are allowed on The Point (the sandy peninsula).

 Pro Tip

Bring cash for entry ($5 an adult, $3 for 62 and older, free for ages 12 and under) and check the county page for boat dock status, since the non-motorized dock has been closed for high water.

No. 05

Austin Paddleboard & Kayak

$$ under 5 min drive Calm kayaking with the dog

A paddle shop tucked into the no-wake zone of the Hurst Creek arm, where boats must idle and the water stays calm. It rents paddle boards and single, double and pedal kayaks, hands out life jackets and paddles, and has plenty of parking and cubbies on the dock for shoes and towels. Weekdays are quieter than weekends. Reservations must be made at least a day ahead; walk-ups are welcome.

Not stated on the shop's own site, which shows a 'dock is closed today' banner. Local press (Community Impact, 2025) says the season is typically March through October with occasional off-season pop-up dates, so expect it closed in most of winter. Check apklt.com before you go.

Dogs may ride on single, double or pedal kayaks on a leash; dogs are not allowed on paddle boards.

 Pro Tip

Stay inside the Hurst Creek arm unless you are an experienced paddler; the main lake has wind and fast boats, and a rescue fee applies if you cannot get back.

Questions about Lake Travis and the water near Lakeway

Can you swim in Lake Travis in winter?

The water is cold in winter, and none of these parks has lifeguards, so swim only in designated areas. Lake levels change what you can reach, and Mansfield Dam Park and Bob Wentz Park close early on winter days.

Are the rental docks open in winter?

Lakeway Marina lists shorter off-season hours but no winter closure dates, so call 512-261-7511 for rental availability. Austin Paddleboard & Kayak is typically open March to October, and its site shows a dock-closed banner now.

Can I bring my dog to the lake?

Lakeway City Park has a fenced Bark Park, and leashed dogs are allowed at Mansfield Dam Park and Bob Wentz Park, but not on The Point. At Austin Paddleboard & Kayak, dogs may ride kayaks on a leash but not paddle boards.

How much does lake access cost?

Lakeway City Park is free. Bob Wentz Park and Mansfield Dam Park are cash only at the entrance. Rental prices at the marina and the paddle shop vary by season, so check their sites.
