Top 5 Coffee Shops
Near Lakeway

Quick coffee, crepes and a few places to settle in.

The best coffee stops near Lakeway are Cafe Lago for a quick breakfast, Cupprimo at the Lake for a cupcake and cold brew, Vivel Crepes & Coffee for brunch, Summer Moon Coffee in Bee Cave for an early drive-thru, and Peaches Coffee for specialty coffee. Drive times run 5 to 20 minutes from the house.

Lakeway is a smaller coffee market than central Austin, so this list mixes cafes, a crepe shop and a drive-thru. Hours and dog notes are on each card where the venue states them. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Cafe Lago

$$ 5 min drive Quick breakfast near the house

A longtime village-square coffeehouse in the Lakeway Village Square plaza, a few minutes from the house. Order a latte or cappuccino with a fresh-baked pastry, or go savory with the gourmet breakfast and daily lunch specials, homemade soups, quiche and deli salads. It is a small, unhurried room that locals treat as a regular stop, and it closes at lunchtime, so plan your coffee run for the morning. Weekend brunch has its own menu.

Mon to Sat 7:30am to 1:30pm, Sun 8am to 1:30pm (per its website).

 Pro Tip

It closes by 1:30 pm every day, so this is a breakfast and early-lunch stop, not an afternoon laptop spot.

No. 02

Cupprimo at the Lake

$$ 10 min drive Cupcake and cold brew treat

The Lakeway outpost of an Austin cupcake bakery that also runs a proper coffee bar. Barrett's Coffee, roasted in Austin, comes as espresso, cold brew, nitro or drip, plus dirty sodas and seasonal drinks, and the cupcakes are baked in house. It opened in January 2026 in the shopping center on RM 620, so it is easy to combine with errands. Park in the center's lot and expect a sweet-tooth crowd with kids on weekends.

 Pro Tip

Get a cold brew or nitro and a cupcake to go for the lake; check cupprimo.com or call for hours first, they were not posted on the site.

No. 03

Vivel Crepes & Coffee

$$ 10 min drive Crepes and coffee brunch

A bright French-leaning cafe in the Oaks at Lakeway on Main Street, good for a sit-down morning start rather than a grab and go. Sweet and savory crepes, a newer French toast and espresso drinks fill the menu, and it also serves lunch and dinner, so it works for a late laptop session or a family brunch. The Main Street center has plenty of parking and a short walk to other shops.

 Pro Tip

Order one sweet and one savory crepe to share with your coffee; weekend mornings fill up, so go early.

No. 04

Summer Moon Coffee (Bee Cave)

$ 15 min drive Early drive-thru, dog-friendly seats

A locally owned Texas roaster known for oak-roasted coffee and its sweet cream Moon Milk, on Highway 71 in Bee Cave. It opens at 6 am, earlier than most nearby cafes, and has a drive-thru for a fast start before a lake day. The pet-friendly outdoor seating is the draw for guests traveling with dogs, and the order to try is the Moon Milk latte, iced or hot.

Daily 6am to 7pm (per its location page).

Pet-friendly outdoor seating, per its location page.

 Pro Tip

Use the drive-thru on a rushed morning, or take your drink to the outdoor seating if you have a dog with you.

No. 05

Peaches Coffee

$$ 20 min drive Specialty coffee before Hamilton Pool

An independent specialty cafe on Hamilton Pool Road, on the way to Hamilton Pool, so it suits a morning that ends at the swimming hole. Espresso drinks include a cinny toast latte and dirty chai, with matcha, muffins and cookies on the side. Parking is a free lot, and it can be a quieter spot to work than the shopping-center chains.

 Pro Tip

Order ahead on Toast from its website, and check hours with the shop before a Sunday visit.

Questions about Lakeway coffee shops

Which Lakeway cafe is closest?

Cafe Lago is the closest, about 5 minutes from the house. It serves breakfast and lunch, open 7:30 am to 1:30 pm Monday to Saturday and 8 am to 1:30 pm on Sunday, so go in the morning.

Where can I get coffee early?

Summer Moon Coffee in Bee Cave opens at 6 am daily, about 15 minutes from the house. Cafe Lago opens at 7:30 am Monday to Saturday and is the closest option.

Is any Lakeway coffee shop dog friendly?

Summer Moon in Bee Cave lists pet-friendly outdoor seating on its own location page. The other four do not state a dog policy on their sites, so call ahead if you plan to bring a dog.

Where can I get brunch near Lakeway?

Vivel Crepes & Coffee serves crepes and coffee about 10 minutes from the house, and Cafe Lago serves breakfast until 1:30 pm. Check each venue's site for current hours before you go.
