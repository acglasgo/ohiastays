Top 5 Grocery Stops
Near Lakeway

Stock the kitchen for a group, with steaks for the grill.

The best grocery stops near Lakeway are H-E-B Lakeway for the weekly shop, Whole Foods Market in Bee Cave for prepared dinners, Trader Joe's in Bee Cave for snacks and wine, Costco South Austin for bulk buys, and Texas Beef Traders for local steaks for the grill. Drive times run 10 to 30 minutes from the house.

A big group with a full kitchen and an outdoor grill needs a good grocery plan. These five cover a weekly shop, ready-made dinners, snacks, a bulk haul and local steaks. Hours come from each store's own site or listing, so confirm holiday hours before you go. We take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

H-E-B Lakeway

$$ 10 min drive week of groceries, ice and charcoal

The nearest full-service grocery to the house and the best first stop for a group of 11. It carries everything for a week of cooking: a full butcher counter with dry-aged beef, a big produce section, bakery, deli and prepared meals, plus the Texas-made Go Texan brands. The Oaks at Lakeway center has plenty of parking, and the H-E-B Fuel station and pharmacy share the lot, so one trip covers groceries, ice, charcoal and a fill-up.

 Pro Tip

Order groceries for curbside pickup before you arrive and grab ice, charcoal and propane-free grilling basics at the front on the way out.

No. 02

Whole Foods Market Bee Cave

$$$ 15 min drive organic, cheese, prepared dinners

The Hill Country Galleria store is the stop for organic produce, a deep natural-foods aisle, a cheese counter with certified cheese professionals, a salad bar and a big prepared-foods section when nobody wants to cook. The meat and seafood counters are good for grill night, and the second-level bar upstairs serves coffee and local beer while you wait on a pickup order. Parking is in the Galleria lot, which fills up on weekend afternoons.

Store site lists Mon 8 am to 9 pm, Tue and Wed 8 am to 10 pm, Fri to Sun 8 am to 9 pm; Thursday currently shows 7 am to 1 pm (Thanksgiving week).

 Pro Tip

Pick up a prepared-foods spread for the arrival-night dinner, then do the bulk shopping at H-E-B or Costco where it costs less.

No. 03

Trader Joe's Bee Cave

$ 15 min drive snacks, frozen apps, wine, cheese

This is the first Trader Joe's in Bee Cave, open since October 2025 in the Shops at the Galleria next to PetSmart. It is a compact store built around affordable private-label snacks, frozen appetizers, cheese, flowers, wine and easy marinated meats that go straight on the grill. Expect a line at the registers on weekends and a smaller cart-friendly layout, so it works best for a top-up trip rather than the main shop for 11.

 Pro Tip

Buy the snacks, frozen appetizers and wine here for the group, and go early on weekdays to skip the checkout line.

No. 04

Costco Wholesale (South Austin)

$$ 30 min drive bulk meat, drinks, group staples

The closest Costco to the house is in south Austin, a longer drive but the right move for a big group on a week-long stay. Buy the bulk basics here: cases of water and drinks, big packs of burgers, chicken, ribs and brisket-ready cuts for the grill, eggs, breakfast items, paper goods and a rotisserie chicken for an easy night. A membership is required to enter, and weekend afternoons are crowded.

Gold Star and Business: Mon to Fri 10 am to 8:30 pm, Sat 9:30 am to 7 pm, Sun 10 am to 6 pm. Closed Thanksgiving Day; Christmas Eve 10 am to 5 pm.

 Pro Tip

Go on a weekday morning and bring your own cooler for the meat, since it is a half-hour drive back to the house.

No. 05

Texas Beef Traders

$$$ 10 min drive local steaks for the grill

A local butcher shop and wine shop in the Boulevard shopping center that sources beef from ranches within about 100 miles. The case holds NY strip, porterhouse and ribeye, 80/20 ground beef, sausages, house-made jerky and cooking tallow, and the Grill Up and Game Day boxes are built for a crowd. Dinners are served Thursday through Sunday evenings if you would rather have someone else cook once.

The Shop: Tue to Sat 11 am to 8 pm, Sun noon to 8 pm, closed Mondays.

 Pro Tip

Call or text a day ahead to reserve porterhouses or a Grill Up box for the group so the case is not sold out.

Questions about Lakeway grocery stops

Which grocery store is closest to the house?

H-E-B Lakeway on RR 620 South and Texas Beef Traders on Lohmans Crossing are both about 10 minutes away. H-E-B is the best all-around store for a big group, and Texas Beef Traders is the butcher for steaks.

What are grocery hours on Thanksgiving week?

Whole Foods Bee Cave shows 7 am to 1 pm on Thursday of Thanksgiving week, and Costco is closed on Thanksgiving Day. H-E-B stores usually close on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, so check heb.com for exact hours.

Where can I buy steaks for the grill?

Texas Beef Traders sells local steaks and is open Tuesday to Saturday 11 am to 8 pm and Sunday noon to 8 pm, closed Mondays. It is about 10 minutes from the house.

Is there a Trader Joe's near Lakeway?

Yes. Trader Joe's Bee Cave opened on Oct 29, 2025, about 15 minutes from the house. Check traderjoes.com for current store hours before you go.
