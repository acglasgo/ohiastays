No. 01

Santa Catarina

$$ 10 min drive regional Mexican for groups

Interior Mexican cooking rather than Tex-Mex, run by two cousins who opened here in 2010. Order the cochinita pibil, the mole dishes or the tostadas topped with avocado and pickled onion, and finish with an espresso martini. The room is bright and relaxed, with tables that work for groups, and it sits in a shopping center off RM 620 with ample lot parking. Sunday opens at 10 am, so it suits a late family brunch.

Mon to Thu 11am-9pm, Fri and Sat 11am-10pm, Sun 10am-9pm (per venue site).

 Pro Tip

Come for Sunday brunch (opens 10 am) when the dinner crowd has not arrived and a table for a big group is easier to get.

No. 02

Hops & Thyme

$$ 10 min drive dog-friendly patio with kids menu

A Lakeway beer garden and brasserie under big live oaks, with a deck and picnic tables built for groups, rotating craft taps and live music on the patio. The kitchen cooks from scratch, with burgers, larger plates and desserts, and there is a children's menu. The main draw for travelers with dogs is one of the largest pet-friendly patios in the area. It sits in The Boulevard shopping center on Lohmans Crossing, with parking out front.

Kitchen 11am-9pm daily; bar open later Thu to Sat (per venue site).

Large pet-friendly patio (Community Impact, 2019); confirm with the restaurant before bringing a dog.

 Pro Tip

Sit at the far end of the patio under the oaks for shade, and check the live-music schedule if you want quiet for young kids; the bar stays open later Thursday to Saturday.

No. 03

Carmela's Lakeway Pizza & Pasteria

$$ 10 min drive pizza and pasta family dinner

Family-owned Italian dinner spot in the Lakeway shopping center at RM 620 and Lohmans, serving Neapolitan-style pizza, penne alla vodka, spaghetti and meatballs, Tuscan blackened salmon and veal or chicken Marsala. Gluten-free pasta is available, and the pizzas are sized for sharing, which works for families with mixed appetites. It is dinner only, Tuesday to Sunday, and closed Mondays. Reservations are available online and the room can get loud near the back.

Tue to Sun 4pm-9pm, closed Mondays (per venue site).

 Pro Tip

Book online ahead for groups and ask for a table away from the back of the room, where reviewers say the music is loudest.

No. 04

LT Corner Pub

$$ 10 min drive Sports, darts, karaoke, late night

A sports pub with an Irish flair tucked into a RR 620 strip center, with ten big-screen TVs, pool, darts, shuffleboard and a back balcony patio. It is a bar first: the website lists karaoke on Wednesday and Friday nights, open mic and darts on other nights, and live music on Saturdays, so the room gets loud and late. The food is pub fare (the site says everything is made from scratch, and its menu page currently reads 'We are updating our menu'); pretzels and queso are pictured on the site.

Site lists open daily 12 pm to 2 am; happy hour 12 to 7 pm Mon to Fri; karaoke Wed and Fri 9 pm to 1 am, live music Sat from 9 pm. The site states no age rule, so ask by phone whether children are allowed and until what hour. Not a kids-friendly evening spot.

Not stated on the venue's website; call to ask whether dogs are allowed on the back patio.

 Pro Tip

This is a bar, not a family restaurant, and its website does not state an age policy or a time when kids must leave. Call (512) 276-2233 before bringing children, and plan to go in the afternoon: karaoke and live music run 9 pm onward and Friday and Saturday hours run to 2 am. Happy hour is 12 to 7 pm on weekdays.

No. 05

Sundancer Grill

$$$ 15 min drive lakeside lunch or sunset dinner

The lake-view pick: a casual lakeside restaurant at the Sail & Ski Yacht Club on Lake Travis, open year-round for lunch, dinner and Sunday brunch. The menu runs to gourmet salads, sandwiches, steak and seafood, with blackened mahi and crab fritters among the house dishes. Boaters can tie up at the courtesy dock (watch shallow water), and a Lake Travis happy hour runs 4 to 6 pm. Car parking is at the yacht club, so allow a few extra minutes to find the entrance.

Mon to Fri 11am-10pm, Sat and Sun 9am-10pm (per venue site).

 Pro Tip

Go for the 4 to 6 pm happy hour to catch the lake in late light before dinner; call ahead on busy weekends, since the site shows no reservation page.

Questions about Lakeway restaurants

Which Lakeway restaurant has a lake view?

Sundancer Grill is the lakeside pick, open 11 am to 10 pm on weekdays and 9 am to 10 pm on weekends, per its site. It is about 15 minutes from the house. Go for lunch or a sunset dinner.

Where can I eat with my dog near Lakeway?

Hops & Thyme has a large pet-friendly patio and a kids menu, according to a 2019 local news report, but its own site does not say so. Call ahead before you bring your dog.

Which restaurants are good for a big group?

Santa Catarina serves regional Mexican for groups, open Monday to Thursday 11 am to 9 pm and Friday and Saturday until 10 pm. Carmela's does pizza and pasta Tuesday to Sunday from 4 pm and is closed Mondays.

Where can I go for a late drink or live music?

LT Corner Pub is open 12 pm to 2 am, with karaoke on Wednesday and Friday from 9 pm and live music on Saturday from 9 pm. It is a bar, so call (512) 276-2233 before you bring children.
