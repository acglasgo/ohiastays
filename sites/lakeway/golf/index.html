Top 5 Golf
Near Lakeway

Public rounds and two private clubs, clearly labeled.

The best golf near Lakeway is Falconhead Golf Club for a public championship round, Pedernales Golf Club for a relaxed nine holes, and Lions Municipal for a historic public round. The Hills Country Club's Live Oak course and Westlake Country Club are private, so you can only play them if a member hosts you.

Golf near Lakeway is mostly private, so each course on this page is marked public or private. Public courses take tee times online or by phone. The private clubs are listed because some local families host guests there, but they are not open to the public and you need a member to invite you. We take no commissions. No. 01

Falconhead Golf Club

$$$ 15 min drive Public championship round, range session

An 18-hole, par-72 daily-fee course in Bee Cave, and the one championship layout in this area that sells tee times to anyone. It has a full practice range and putting green, so you can turn up without a membership. Book a tee time online or by phone; expect a premium public price and a busy tee sheet on weekends.

Golf shop daily 6 am to 6 pm; driving range sunrise to sunset (last bucket 5 pm), closes 3 pm Tuesdays and opens 10 am Wednesdays. Closes when rain shuts the course.

 Pro Tip

Anyone can book a tee time (online through the club's site or by phone). The club asks you not to double-book overlapping times at its sister courses or the reservation is cancelled. If you only want to hit balls, the range is open without a tee time.

No. 02

The Hills Country Club: Live Oak Course (private, formerly Lakeway Country Club)

$$$$ 10 min drive Member-hosted round, walkable classic course Private club

Private club: this is not a public course, and a guest can only play if a member hosts them. Live Oak is an 18-hole, par-72 walkable layout of 6,808 yards, known for mature live oaks, winding fairways and recently renovated greens, and it rewards precision over power. It sits inside The Hills Country Club, whose membership covers four courses. If you have a friend or neighbor who is a member, ask whether they would host you; the club handles guest fees and its own rules.

Private: members and their accompanied or pre-registered guests only, per the club's own site; not open to the public.

Private: members and member-hosted guests only. The club's site lists no hours; ask your member host or call the golf shop.

 Pro Tip

You cannot book this course yourself. Only play if a member invites you and registers you as a guest; the club notes proper golf attire is required and soft-spike shoes only. Do not turn up and ask for a tee time. Guest fees are set by the club and are not published, so ask your member host.

No. 03

Pedernales Golf Club (Willie Nelson's Cut-N-Putt)

$ 30 min drive Relaxed nine holes, Willie fans

Willie Nelson's own nine-hole course out past Spicewood, par 36 with a driving range, chipping area and putting green. Membership is optional and the course is open to the public. The club's tongue-in-cheek house rules set a relaxed tone, so it suits a casual nine, a lesson, or a range session rather than a serious round. It is also the farthest pick here, about a half hour from the house.

 Pro Tip

Open to the public, no membership needed. Online tee times appear on GolfNow, but they are often not posted, so call the pro shop to confirm a time and the current fee (the club's site lists no prices).

No. 04

Westlake Country Club (private)

$$$$ 30 min drive Member-hosted round, renovated course Private club

Private club: guests can only play if a member hosts them, and there is no public booking. Westlake is an 18-hole, par-71, 6,935-yard course by Lanny Wadkins, reimagined in a major renovation (formerly Lost Creek Country Club). The site also lists a renovated clubhouse, pools, tennis and pickleball for members. Choose it only if a member friend invites you; it is a farther drive than the Hills clubs.

Private: members and their accompanied or pre-registered guests only, per the club's own FAQ; select tournaments and outings may be open to guests. Not bookable by the public.

Private: members and member-hosted guests only. The club's site lists no hours; ask your member host or call the club.

 Pro Tip

Never assume you can book a tee time here. A member has to host and register you; ask the host about guest fees and dress rules. Public outings and select tournaments sometimes admit guests, so ask the club if you want to join one.

No. 05

Lions Municipal Golf Course (the Muny)

$$ 35 min drive Historic public round near downtown

Austin's city-owned 18-hole course, opened in 1924 as the city's first public course and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Cisco's Muny Cafe is on site, and the driving range (irons only) and putting greens are open to visitors. Open to everyone; book online with a free city account, or by phone or in person at the pro shop. It is about 35 minutes from the house.

Closed the third Monday of each month (the Dec 21 date applies), plus scheduled aerifying days; driving range (irons only) closes Mondays at 1 pm. Book sunset rounds from the city's seasonal chart.

The city's cart-fee page lists pets as non-golfer riders ($10); ask the pro shop before bringing a dog.

 Pro Tip

Weekday online times open at 9 am seven days ahead; weekend times release the Monday before at 8 pm. Card only, no cash. Some GolfNow cart-included rates run roughly $30 to $46 a golfer, so compare both before booking.

Questions about golf near Lakeway

Which golf courses near Lakeway are open to the public?

Falconhead, Pedernales Golf Club and Lions Municipal are open to the public. Falconhead books tee times online or by phone, and Lions Municipal books online with a free city account, by phone or in person. Call Pedernales to confirm tee times and fees.

Can I play the private clubs near Lakeway?

Only if a member hosts you. The Hills Country Club, which includes the Live Oak and Yaupon courses, and Westlake Country Club allow members and their guests. Ask a host who is a member before you plan a round.

Is there a driving range nearby?

Falconhead's driving range needs no tee time and runs sunrise to sunset, with the last bucket at 5 pm, and it closes at 3 pm on Tuesdays. Lions Municipal also has a range, for irons only.

Are any courses closed on certain days?

Lions Municipal closes the third Monday of each month, including Dec 21, 2026, plus scheduled aerifying days. Check each course's site before you drive.
