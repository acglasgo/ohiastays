Top 5 Coffee Shops
in Austin

Patios, food trucks and plenty of room for the dog.

The best coffee shops near downtown Austin for a South Austin stay are Summer Moon Coffee for Moon Milk lattes, Jo's Coffee on South Congress, Buzz Mill Coffee for 24-hour hours and a patio, Radio Coffee & Beer for an outdoor lounge, and Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden for food trucks. Drive times run 5 to 10 minutes from the house.

Coffee in South Austin comes with patios, food trucks and dogs under the table. These five are the shops we use ourselves, for a morning start or an afternoon of laptop work. Hours and dog notes are on each card. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Summer Moon Coffee (South First Original)

$ 5 min drive Moon Milk lattes, dog patio, laptop work

The original Summer Moon, opened on South 1st in a low strip building just south of the river, is known for oak-roasted coffee and Moon Milk, a sweet cream that sits in lattes and cold drinks. Order at the walk-up window or inside, then claim a table on the pet-friendly patio. Free wifi and indoor seating make it a workable morning stop. Parking is a small shared lot out front, so expect to circle at peak times.

Mon-Fri 6 am to 7 pm, Sat-Sun 7 am to 7 pm (per the venue's location page).

Pet-friendly patio seating (per the venue's own location page).

 Pro Tip

Try the Moon Milk drink iced; the walk-up window is quicker than the indoor line.

No. 02

Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden (Pickle Road)

$$ 5 min drive garden patio, food trucks, remote work

A shaded garden off South Congress with a big deck, string lights, a chicken coop and a pond, where coffee in the morning turns into local beer and cocktails later. Food trucks park on site (Pueblo Viejo, Biggie's Yardbird and Wow Poke were the featured trucks after the late-2025 renovation), and the indoor room has outlets and long tables for laptops. Note the 21+ after 7 pm policy, so go earlier if you have kids.

Sun-Wed 8 am to 10 pm, Thu-Sat 8 am to midnight; 21+ after 7 pm (per the venue's group site).

Dogs reported welcome inside and out by The Infatuation (https://theinfatuation.com/austin/reviews/cosmic-coffee-beer-garden); the venue's own site does not state a policy for this location, so confirm.

 Pro Tip

Come before 7 pm: the venue goes 21+ after that, and weekday mornings are the quietest for working.

No. 03

Buzz Mill Coffee

$ 10 min drive 24-hour coffee, patio, tacos

A rough-and-ready East Riverside coffee shop and bar that never closes, with a gravel patio of blue picnic tables, string lights and a cedar-post fence. Coffee, beer and cider come from the counter, and the Santo Patio taco trailer on site runs 8 am to 10 pm daily. Open mic nights run on Mondays. It is the only true 24-hour option on this list, so it suits early flights and late arrivals; street parking is easy.

Open 24 hours; Santo Patio food trailer 8 am to 10 pm daily (per the venue's site).

Dogs reported welcome on the patio, with water bowls, by Sidewalk Dog (https://sidewalkdog.com/articles/dog-friendly-coffee-shops-austin); the venue's own site does not state a policy, so confirm.

 Pro Tip

Grab breakfast tacos from the Santo Patio trailer and eat them on the patio.

No. 04

Radio Coffee & Beer (Radio South)

$$ 10 min drive outdoor lounge, live music, coffee

The original Radio, open since 2014, is a South Austin yard of picnic tables under big trees and string lights, with an espresso bar, local craft beer and cocktails. Live music and trivia fill the evenings, and food trucks (Shortwave, Veracruz, Wow Poke and Briscuits are listed on its site) park on the lot. Mornings are calm and daylight is the best time to bring kids.

Mon-Sat 7 am to midnight, Sun 7 am to 11 pm (per the venue's site).

Dogs reported welcome on the shaded patio and yard by local dog-friendly guides (https://austin.culturemap.com/news/restaurants-bars/dog-friendly-restaurants-austin); the venue's own site does not state a policy, so confirm.

 Pro Tip

Check the site's show calendar; music nights are lively and crowded, so come before dinner for a quieter patio.

No. 05

Jo's Coffee (South Congress)

$ 10 min drive iconic mural, dog-friendly, quick coffee

The walk-up corner stand with the red 'i love you so much' mural, steps from the South Congress shops and about two miles from downtown. Order at the window, then sit at the shaded outdoor tables, which fill with people-watchers all day. The menu is coffee plus a short food list, and dogs are a regular sight. Street parking nearby can be tight on weekends, so walk or rideshare from a nearby lot if you can.

Daily 7 am to 7 pm (per the venue's site).

Dogs 'always welcome' per Time Out (https://www.timeout.com/austin/restaurants/jos-hot-coffee-good-food); the venue's own site does not state a policy.

 Pro Tip

Take your coffee for a walk down South Congress; weekday mornings avoid the crowds.

Questions about Austin coffee shops

Which Austin coffee shop is open 24 hours?

Buzz Mill Coffee on Town Creek Drive is open 24 hours, with a patio and the Santo Patio food trailer serving from 8 am to 10 pm daily.

Where can I work remotely over coffee?

Summer Moon Coffee on South First has free wifi and a patio, and Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden is a good spot to work. Radio Coffee & Beer is better for lounging, since its wifi policy is unclear and outlets are limited.

Are these Austin coffee shops dog friendly?

Summer Moon's own site says its patio is pet-friendly. Cosmic, Buzz Mill, Radio and Jo's are reported dog friendly by local guides but do not state a policy on their own sites, so confirm before you go.

Is Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden open to kids at night?

Cosmic is 21 and over after 7 pm, per its group site. Bring kids in the morning or afternoon, when the food trucks and garden are open to everyone.
