Top 5 Grocery Stops
Near South Austin

Where to stock the kitchen, from a weekly shop to a local-foods stop.

The best grocery stops for a South Austin stay are H-E-B at Slaughter and South Congress for the weekly shop, Whole Foods Market's Lamar flagship for ready-made dinners, Central Market Westgate for cheese and wine, Costco South Austin for bulk buys, and Wheatsville Co-op for local Texas foods. Drive times run under 5 to 15 minutes from the house.

A full kitchen makes a grocery run worth it. These five cover a weekly shop, a splurge dinner, a bulk haul and a local-foods stop. Hours come from each store's own site or listing, so confirm holiday hours before you go. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Whole Foods Market Lamar (Flagship)

$$$ 15 min drive ready-made dinners and local goods

The original Whole Foods flagship, in the building that also houses the company's headquarters, is a destination as much as a store. Wander the sprawling prepared-foods hall, the salad and hot bars, the bakery, the meat and seafood counters and a deep local-products section, then eat on the spot or load the cooler for the house. It suits a first-night shop of ready-to-heat dinners, breakfast supplies and Texas snacks. Parking is in the garage under the building, and the store is busiest on weekends around noon.

Store site lists 7 am to 10 pm daily; Thanksgiving Day (Thu Nov 26) shows 7 am to 1 pm.

 Pro Tip

This flagship is on the north edge of downtown, roughly 15 minutes from the South Austin home, so pair it with a downtown outing rather than a special trip. For a quick stock-up closer to the house, use the H-E-B on S Congress.

No. 02

H-E-B Slaughter and South Congress

$$ under 5 min drive main weekly grocery run

This is Texas's hometown grocer and the practical choice for filling a full kitchen. Expect a wide produce section, a Texas-sized meat counter, fresh tortillas, a bakery, a pharmacy and the H-E-B house brands, which are good and cheaper than the national names. Pick up breakfast tacos fixings, brisket-friendly rubs and Texas beer in one stop. Parking is a large surface lot and the store opens early and closes late, so it works around any arrival time.

Store listing shows 6 am to 11 pm daily; confirm holiday hours on heb.com.

 Pro Tip

This is the H-E-B closest to the South Austin home, a few minutes' drive down S Congress, so make it your main stock-up. Use H-E-B curbside pickup on arrival day if you land late.

No. 03

Central Market Westgate

$$$ 10 min drive cheese, wine and dinner-party shopping

H-E-B's upscale sibling is where to shop when the group wants a nicer spread. The cheese and charcuterie counters, wine and craft beer wall, deep produce and seafood, and a big bulk-foods section make it easy to build a dinner-party menu. There is also an in-store cafe for a quick bite while you shop. The store has had a recent remodel with wider aisles and faster checkout, and its lot is easy to park in.

H-E-B's store listing shows 8 am to 10 pm daily; confirm holiday hours.

 Pro Tip

This Westgate store is the Central Market closest to the South Austin home (the other is on N Lamar). Come here for cheese, wine and special-occasion ingredients, and do the everyday basics at H-E-B.

No. 04

Costco Wholesale South Austin

$$ 10 min drive bulk buys for a big group

For a big group or a long stay, this warehouse is the bulk answer: cases of water and drinks, party-size meats, rotisserie chickens, fresh produce, paper goods and a wine and spirits aisle. The food court is a cheap lunch break, and the pharmacy and tire center are on site. It does require a Costco membership, so confirm someone in your party has one before you drive over. Expect crowds on weekend afternoons.

Site lists Mon to Fri 10 am to 8:30 pm, Sat 9:30 am to 7 pm, Sun 10 am to 6 pm (Executive members open earlier); closed Thanksgiving Day.

 Pro Tip

This is the Costco closest to the South Austin home, about 10 minutes west on William Cannon. Go on a weekday morning to skip the lines, and bring your membership card.

No. 05

Wheatsville Co-op (South Lamar)

$$$ 10 min drive local Texas foods and deli

Austin's community-owned co-op has been feeding locals since 1976, and this South Lamar store is its main location. It is the local specialty stop: a strong deli with grab-and-go meals, a Local Vendors section stocking Texas-made foods, plus organic produce, natural groceries and craft beer. Good for a small, friendly shop of regional items rather than a full stock-up. It stays open from 7 am to 10 pm, handy for early breakfast runs.

Store site lists 7 am to 10 pm.

 Pro Tip

This South Lamar store is the Wheatsville closest to the South Austin home, about 10 minutes away. Order the deli's Popcorn Tofu for pickup if you want dinner without cooking.

Questions about Austin grocery stops

Which grocery store is closest to South Austin?

H-E-B at Slaughter and South Congress is the closest of these five, under 5 minutes from the house. Its store listing shows 6 am to 11 pm daily, so confirm holiday hours on heb.com.

What are grocery hours on Thanksgiving in Austin?

Whole Foods Lamar lists 7 am to 1 pm on Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26, and Costco is closed that day. H-E-B and Central Market hours vary around the holiday, so check heb.com first.

Where should I shop for a big group?

Costco South Austin is the bulk option and needs a membership. H-E-B is the best all-around store for a weekly shop, and Central Market is the place for cheese, wine and a dinner-party spread.

Where can I buy local Texas foods?

Wheatsville Co-op on South Lamar focuses on local Texas foods and has a deli. Its site lists 7 am to 10 pm daily.
