Around the House

The living room has an 84-inch pull-down projector screen with a Roku, plus a rolling TV. Movie night is easy:

  • Step 1: lower the projector screen and turn the projector on with the power button on the projector remote.
  • Step 2: use the dial on the projector remote to switch to HDMI 1, which is the Roku player.
  • Step 3: sign in to Roku. When it asks, enter your check-out date so your data is cleared and stays private, then pick your favorite app and sign in.

If the picture does not fit the screen: open the settings gear on the projector home screen, choose Projection Image Setting, then Manual Keystone. Use the arrow keys to move each corner of the picture to the corner of the screen and press OK to switch corners. Press the back button when the green circle looks as round as possible.

The full kitchen is ready for group meals, with a coffee machine and a large water purifier in the fridge. Fresh towels and linens are provided. There are indoor and outdoor dining spaces, and board games for rainy days.

High vaulted ceilings, a comfortable sofa and a sofa bed make the living room the heart of the house. Gather for a movie on the projector, or play a board game at the dining table.

The private fenced backyard has a grassy space for dogs to play, a cozy sitting area with string lights, and a fire pit for starlit evenings.

Please never leave a fire unattended, put it out completely before you go inside, and follow any local burn bans.

Primary bedroom: a queen bed with soft linens and its own bathroom with a walk-in shower.

Bedroom 2: another queen bed with soft linens.

Bedroom 3: a twin-over-full bunk bed, great for kids and groups. Bunk bed use is at your own risk, so children must be supervised by an adult at all times, and please follow the weight limits.

The living-room sofa also folds out into a bed for an extra guest.

There are 2 full bathrooms. The primary bathroom has a walk-in shower, and the hall bathroom has a tub and shower combination. Shampoo, conditioner, body soap, shower gel and a hair dryer are provided.

A washer is available for your stay, along with a clothes drying rack.
