Top 5 Restaurants
in Austin

Brisket, Tex-Mex and easy family dinners, all close to South Congress.

The best restaurants near downtown Austin for a South Austin stay are Franklin Barbecue for brisket, Güero's Taco Bar and Chuy's for Tex-Mex, Home Slice Pizza for an easy family dinner, and P. Terry's Burger Stand for quick, cheap burgers. All five are 10 to 15 minutes' drive from the house.

These are the places we send friends and family when they visit: one famous barbecue line, two Tex-Mex classics, a pizza counter and a fast burger stand. Two sit on South Congress, so they pair well with a stroll. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions from any of them. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Franklin Barbecue

$$ 10 min drive the famous Texas brisket

The brisket that put Austin barbecue on the world map: post oak smoke, a peppery bark and fat that gives way under a plastic knife. Order by the half pound at the counter, add a rib or turkey and a side of pinto beans, and eat at shared picnic tables. Walk-up service means a morning line, so most guests pre-order online or arrive early with chairs and a thermos. It sells out most afternoons and kids are welcome.

Tuesday to Sunday 11am until sold out (around 2 or 3pm); Monday closed. Closed Nov 26-27 and Dec 25-28.

Friendly dogs are allowed on the patio.

 Pro Tip

Use the Order in Advance link on the Franklin site to skip the line, or send one person early while the family sleeps in.

No. 02

Güero's Taco Bar

$$ 10 min drive Tex-Mex and live music

A South Congress Tex-Mex institution since 1986, set in a converted feed store with a bar that feels like an old Mexican cantina. Order the hand-shaken margaritas, tacos and enchiladas, and ask for a table in the Oak Garden, where live music plays under big oaks from Wednesday to Sunday. There is a kids menu, and waits are common at dinner, so the garden and bar make the wait easy.

11am to 10pm Wednesday to Saturday, 11am to 9pm Sunday to Tuesday; closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

 Pro Tip

Reserve through OpenTable on gueros.com for dinner, or put your name in and wait in the Oak Garden with a drink.

No. 03

Chuy's (original, Barton Springs Rd)

$$ 15 min drive Tex-Mex with kids

The original Chuy's opened on Barton Springs Road in 1982 and still has the over-the-top Elvis-and-hubcap decor. Go for Tex-Mex plates such as the green chile fried chicken enchiladas, chile rellenos and the creamy jalapeno dip with chips, then finish with a margarita. It is loud, busy and easy with kids. Expect a wait at peak times, and the back patio is the spot when the weather is nice.

 Pro Tip

Dinner waits stretch on weekends; go early or at an off hour, and ask for a patio table.

No. 04

Home Slice Pizza

$$ 10 min drive easy family pizza night

New York style pizza by the pie or the slice, a short walk from the shops of South Congress. The thin, foldable slices are big enough for a meal; order a plain cheese or pepperoni for picky kids and something with spinach and ricotta for adults. The room is loud, casual and often crowded, so go before the dinner rush. Extra parking is in the church lot off Elizabeth Street.

11am to 11pm Sunday to Thursday, 11am to midnight Friday and Saturday (holiday hours may differ).

 Pro Tip

Park in the church lot off Elizabeth Street (the restaurant says no donation is required) rather than circling South Congress.

No. 05

P. Terry's Burger Stand (S. Lamar)

$ 15 min drive quick cheap burgers with kids

The original P. Terry's stand on South Lamar, a drive-thru and walk-up burger counter that locals treat as a staple. Order a burger with fries and a hand-dipped shake, or the vegetarian Impossible-style options if someone in the group does not eat beef. It is fast, cheap and good with kids after a long day, and the company says this original location now serves around the clock, which helps with late arrivals.

Company press release (pterrys.com, June 2025) says the original South Lamar stand is open 24 hours.

Time Out Austin notes the stand offers dog treats (not confirmed on the venue's own site).

 Pro Tip

The original stand is open 24 hours per the company's June 2025 announcement, so it is a reliable late-arrival dinner; there is also a P. Terry's on W William Cannon near the rental.

Questions about Austin restaurants

Does Franklin Barbecue sell out?

Yes. Franklin serves Tuesday to Sunday from 11 am until the meat sells out, usually around 2 or 3 pm, and it is closed Mondays. It is also closed Nov 26 to 27 and Dec 25 to 28, so plan to join the line early.

Which Austin restaurants are good with kids?

Chuy's, Home Slice Pizza and P. Terry's Burger Stand are all casual and quick, which makes them easy with kids. Güero's is lively and family-welcome, with live music on its Oak Garden Wednesday to Sunday.

Can I bring my dog to dinner in Austin?

Franklin Barbecue allows friendly dogs on its patio, per its own site. The other four places do not state a dog policy on their own sites, so call ahead before you bring a dog.

Are these restaurants open on holidays?

Not always. Güero's is closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, and Franklin has the closures above. Home Slice posts holiday hours for Dec 24 to 25, so confirm by phone.
