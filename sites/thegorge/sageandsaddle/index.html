Welcome to The Gorge

We are so glad you are here. This sleek one-bedroom cabin at Sagecliffe sleeps up to 4, with a coffee bar, a grill and long views over the vineyard to the Columbia River.

Cave B Estate Winery is about 2 minutes away and the Gorge Amphitheatre is about a 10-minute walk. Here is how everything in the cabin works, with our favorite places to explore after that.

Essential Info

4 PM check-in. This home has self check-in with a smart lock, so no physical keys are needed. You can arrive on your own schedule after 4 PM.

Your door code, WiFi details and the address arrive in your check-in message, 3 days before check-in.

Hoping to arrive earlier? Message us through your booking app and we will do our best.

Free parking is available on site.

A car is the easiest way to get around, since this is a rural spot. Cave B Estate Winery is about 2 minutes away, the Gorge Amphitheatre is about a 10-minute walk, and the restaurants and grocery stores in Quincy are about a 20-minute drive.

WiFi details are in your check-in message. The connection is fast, which makes the dining table a handy spot to work.

You will find the remote for the heating and cooling unit on the wall to the right.

Tips: please keep all windows and outside doors closed while the heat or A/C is running so it works efficiently. We recommend a setting of 72F, which keeps the cabin comfortable.

Quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM. This is a friendly community full of other people's homes, so please keep noise to a minimum and respect your neighbors' peace.

There are security cameras at the entrances.

10 AM check-out. Before you head out:

  • Put trash in the bins.
  • Lights and TVs off.
  • Lock any doors and windows you opened during your stay.

Thank you for taking care of our home. Safe travels!

This cabin welcomes up to 4 guests. Children and infants are welcome.

No parties or events. This is a smoke-free home, including the outside area around the property, and that covers smoking or vaping of any substance.

Pets are welcome as booked. Please clean up after your pet.

If something breaks during your stay, please tell us through your booking app so we can repair or replace it for the next guest.

It is breezy here, so secure your hats and anything light. Rattlesnakes and ticks live in this part of the Gorge too, so use bug spray on the trails and give any rattlesnake room to move along.

The cabin relies on a shared community water and septic system, so the hosts cannot control occasional maintenance or service interruptions. If something stops working, message us through your booking app and we will work with the community to get it fixed quickly.

Dish soap is under the kitchen sink, and any extra extension cord is in the cabinets to the right of the front door.

In an emergency, call 911.

Around the House

The full kitchen has a stove and oven, microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator and everything you need to cook, plus a coffee bar to start your morning. There is a 4-person dining table for post-winery dinners and pre-concert meals, with a 4K TV on the wall.

The gray sectional is the heart of the cabin, with glass sliding doors that open to the porch and long views of the vineyard and the river.

The couches can be set up as two twin beds. You can make them up yourselves with the extra sheets and bedding stored in the closets by the front door.

The bedroom has a king bed with quality linens, a stand-up mirror and a hair dryer. Fresh towels and bed linens are provided.

The bathroom has a walk-in shower, and shampoo, conditioner, lotion and luxury hand soap are provided.

The washing machine is in the main hallway just before the bathroom. Press the power button and add detergent: pods go inside the drum and powdered detergent goes in the dispenser. There is also a magnetic remote that stays on top of the washer.

An outdoor grill and a bench sit just outside the sliding doors, with views across the vineyard to the Columbia River.

Tip: please leave the grill clean after use so it is ready for the next cook. Grill use is at your own risk.

Cave B Estate Winery is about 2 minutes away for tastings and sunsets, and the Gorge Amphitheatre is about a 10-minute walk, with concerts all summer. Tendrils Restaurant and the Sagecliffe Spa are available with reservations.
