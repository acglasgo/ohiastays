Top 5 Columbia River
& Water Spots

Boat launches, beaches and rentals, with honest winter notes.

The best river and water spots near The Gorge are Sunland Estates Boat Launch for a quiet river launch, Quincy Lakes for paddling and fishing, Wanapum Recreation Area for a family swim beach, Crescent Bar Recreation Area for summer beach days, and Columbia Jetskis for rentals. Most are seasonal, so check each card for winter closures.

The Columbia River is close, but this is mostly a May to September page. In winter the ramps are quiet, rentals are closed and the wind is real. Each card says what is seasonal. We take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Sunland Estates Boat Launch

$ 15 min drive Quiet river launch near the house

The closest public water to the house: a WDFW boat ramp on the Columbia River (Lake Wanapum) just west of George, in the Quincy Lakes wildlife area. It is a working launch, not a resort beach, so expect sage and basalt, big sky and a quiet shoreline. Bring your own boat, kayak or paddleboards, or come to fish and watch the cliffs change colour at sunset. Facilities are minimal, so pack water, sun cover and a trash bag, and a Discover Pass goes on the dash.

WDFW's page lists no hours or seasonal closure for this ramp; a Discover Pass or Vehicle Access Pass is required on WDFW land. Winter wind, ice and low light are real, so check conditions first.

 Pro Tip

This is the Quincy-area Sunland, about 8 miles west of George. It is not the Sunland near Chelan or Wenatchee, so type the address above rather than the name into your GPS.

No. 02

Wanapum Recreation Area

$ 35 min drive Family swim beach and picnic

A Washington State Parks site on Lake Wanapum at Vantage, with a swim beach, a double-lane boat ramp, docks, grass and shade trees on a long sage-brown shoreline. It is the easiest family pick on this list because there are restrooms and showers, picnic space and room to spread out. There is no lifeguard on duty, so keep a close eye on children. The campground is closed in winter, but day use stays available.

Summer 6:30 a.m. to dusk, winter 8 a.m. to dusk (park site). Wanapum campground closed Nov 1 to March 1; day use stays open.

Dogs are allowed on a leash (Washington State Parks).

 Pro Tip

Buy the $10 day Discover Pass before you leave. Boat launches add a $7 day fee. While Vantage Road SW is closed (WDFW notes a rockslide), follow your GPS's current routing.

No. 03

Columbia Jetskis (Crescent Bar)

$$$ 45 min drive Jet skis and pontoon party barges

The host's pick is a real, local rental business on Crescent Bar island, renting jet skis, pontoon boats (party barges), kayaks, paddleboards and golf carts, so you can cover the river in an afternoon. Pontoons suit groups and kids, and jet skis suit thrill seekers. The island has a marina, a pizza window, ice cream and a general store. The website is taking 2027 reservations. Prices, minimum ages and season dates are not published, so call before you drive.

The business's own site gives no season or hours. A directory listing shows 9 am to 6 pm, and is not the business's own statement. Treat it as a May to September summer operation and call first. Not operating in winter.

 Pro Tip

Book ahead for summer weekends, and bring your Washington boater education card if you were born in 1955 or later, since it is required to run a personal watercraft. Rentals can be cancelled for sustained wind over 20 mph, per the site's policies.

No. 04

Crescent Bar Recreation Area

Free 45 min drive Summer beach, tubing and boats

Grant PUD's river island resort between the Columbia and a channel, with boat ramps, a marina with day-use docks and the only on-water fuel around, beaches, parks with reservable shelters, and a 9-hole golf course along the river. Summer is the draw: tubing, wakeboarding, swimming and lawn time at Riverbend and Chinook parks. The pace is busy on weekends and quiet in winter, when most services close.

Park and launches open 7 AM to 10 PM; the marina and fuel run May 1 to Sept 30 (2024 Grant PUD documents; confirm for the current year). The office closes early and was closed for Thanksgiving week. Golf is open in winter when weather allows, but only above 40 F.

 Pro Tip

Parking is free from 7 AM to 10 PM and overnight parking is prohibited. Launching is free. Shelters at Riverbend or Chinook Park are $50 a day for groups under 35.

No. 05

Quincy Lakes (Burke Lake and Quincy Lake)

$ 20 min drive Quiet paddling and fishing lakes

A WDFW wildlife area of small lakes cut into basalt channels north-east of the house, with boat ramps at Burke Lake, Quincy Lake, Stan Coffin Lake and Evergreen Reservoir. It is a place for paddling and fishing rather than swimming, with calm water, cliffs and wetlands. There are no campgrounds and no services, only dispersed sites, so bring everything and take it out. The lakes are best from spring to early fall.

Foot access is open year-round, but WDFW closes the main access road to vehicles from Oct 1 to late Feb or early March, and Burke and Quincy lakes are listed as seasonal trout waters, open Mar 1 to Jul 31 (Bureau of Reclamation). Evergreen Reservoir's ramp is typically usable from late March to mid-October. A rockslide has closed Vantage Road SW.

 Pro Tip

A Discover Pass is required and can be bought at the automated pay station. No campfires in the Ancient Lakes area.

Questions about the Columbia River and water near The Gorge

Can I swim or paddle in winter?

Not comfortably. Boating and swimming are really a May to September pleasure here, and Columbia Jetskis does not operate in winter. Wanapum's day-use area stays open, but it has no lifeguard.

Which spots are open now?

Wanapum Recreation Area's day use is open year-round, from 8 am to dusk in winter. Crescent Bar's marina and fuel run May 1 to Sept 30 per its operator, so winter services are reduced.

Do I need a pass for the boat launches?

A Discover Pass or Vehicle Access Pass is required on state wildlife land, which includes Sunland Estates Boat Launch. Check each managing agency's page for current requirements.

Can I bring my dog to the river?

Dogs are allowed on a leash at Wanapum Recreation Area, per Washington State Parks. The other cards do not state a dog rule, so check with the managing agency first.
