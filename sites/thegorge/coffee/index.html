Top 5 Coffee Shops
Near The Gorge

A drive into Quincy for coffee, cinnamon rolls and early lattes.

The best coffee stops near The Gorge are Murphy's Corner Coffee for cinnamon rolls and breakfast, Stick & Rudder in the Quincy Public Market for locally roasted coffee, Gotta Have Java for an early latte, Daily Brew Drive Thru for a quick to-go drink, and Whipped for sweet specialty drinks. Drive times run 20 to 30 minutes from the house.

There is little near the house, so coffee means a drive into Quincy. Hours come from each shop's own site or listings, and a few shops have no website, so call to confirm. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Murphy's Corner Coffee Co.

$ 20 min drive cinnamon rolls and easy breakfast

A restored old gas station on Highway 283 near George, now a country-style coffee shop with a covered porch and picnic seating, and the closest good cup to the house. The menu runs from espresso, iced coffee, frappes and milkshakes to breakfast burritos, sandwiches and a turkey club, with pastries baked fresh each morning. The cinnamon rolls are the signature. Beans are roasted in Ellensburg. It sits right on the road, so pulling in is easy with a trailer or a full car.

Closed Mon; Tue to Sat 7 am to 2 pm; Sun 9 am to 1:30 pm (per its own site)

Dogs welcome outdoors only, leashed; none inside (per its own site)

 Pro Tip

Closed Mondays, and it closes at 2 pm most days, so go for breakfast, not an afternoon latte. Grab a cinnamon roll early.

No. 02

Stick & Rudder Coffee Bar & Roaster

$ 30 min drive roasted-in-town coffee, weekday stop

An aviation-themed espresso bar and small-batch roaster tucked inside the Quincy Public Market, a big industrial hall of local vendors, with the roasting done on site. Order the classic espresso drinks and take a bag of its beans home. The market's other counters, including a bakery stall, are steps away, and there is room to sit and linger. It is a weekday spot, so plan it for a Friday departure or a midweek errand.

Mon to Fri 7 am to 5 pm (per the Quincy Public Market merchant page; weekends not listed)

 Pro Tip

Open weekdays only; skip it on a Saturday or Sunday. Pair it with a walk through the market and a browse of the local wine shop next door.

No. 03

Gotta Have Java

$ 25 min drive early weekday latte to go

A small drive-thru coffee hut in the middle of Quincy that has been pouring since 2011, with an early weekday start at 5:30 am for anyone heading out before the Gorge fills up. Order a latte or an Americano at the walk-up or drive-up window; reviewers praise the drinks as consistently good and the service fast. There is one outdoor table, so plan on taking your cup to go. Parking is simple on E Street, and the window handles a trailer-pulling line without fuss.

Mon to Fri 5:30 am to 6 pm; Sat 7 am to 4 pm; Sun 7 am to 3 pm (per Yelp/Restaurantji listings; no own site, so call to confirm)

One outdoor table; listed as pet-friendly by BringFido (not confirmed by the shop)

 Pro Tip

It opens at 5:30 am on weekdays, earlier than most Quincy spots, so it suits a pre-dawn start; tip the barista, as reviewers ask.

No. 04

Daily Brew Drive Thru

$ 25 min drive quick to-go latte

A friendly drive-through coffee stand on F Street in the middle of Quincy, with a loyal local following and an early start for anyone heading to the road or the field. Expect a full espresso menu, blended drinks and quick, cheerful service from the window. It is the practical pick for a to-go latte before a day at the Gorge or a long drive. There is no seating, so plan to drink it in the car or at a park.

 Pro Tip

The shop's old website is offline, so check its Facebook page for the day's hours before you go.

No. 05

Whipped

$ 25 min drive sweet specialty drinks, Mexican-inspired

A family-run drive-through that opened in 2021 with a bilingual menu and Mexican-inspired recipes, and it now has a mobile coffee truck too. It leans toward sweeter, topped-and-whipped specialty drinks, so it is a good stop when a plain drip will not do. Expect a line of locals at the window rather than a place to sit, so treat it as a grab-and-go stop on the way through town.

 Pro Tip

Try one of the Mexican-inspired specialty drinks, and ask if the mobile truck is parked somewhere closer when you go.

Questions about coffee near The Gorge

Where can I get coffee near Cave B?

The closest shops are in Quincy, about 20 to 30 minutes from the house. Murphy's Corner Coffee at 1099 WA-283 is the nearest at 20 minutes, open Tuesday to Saturday 7 am to 2 pm and Sunday 9 am to 1:30 pm.

Which coffee shop opens earliest?

Gotta Have Java lists weekday hours from 5:30 am, but it has no website, so call to confirm. Murphy's Corner opens at 7 am Tuesday to Saturday and is closed on Mondays.

Can I bring my dog to a coffee shop here?

Murphy's Corner welcomes leashed dogs outdoors only, per its own site. Gotta Have Java has one outdoor table and is listed as pet-friendly by BringFido, though the shop has not confirmed it.

Which coffee shops are open on weekends?

Murphy's Corner is open Saturday and Sunday. Stick & Rudder lists weekday hours only. Gotta Have Java lists Saturday 7 am to 4 pm and Sunday 7 am to 3 pm in directory listings.
