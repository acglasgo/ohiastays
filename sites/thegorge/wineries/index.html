Top 5 Tasting Rooms
Near The Gorge

A walkable tasting at Cave B, plus wine country and cider farther out.

The best tasting rooms near The Gorge are Cave B Estate Winery for a walkable tasting with river views, Beaumont Cellars for a quiet estate tasting, Jones of Washington in the Quincy Public Market, Discovery Cider in Ephrata for local cider, and Gård Public House in Royal City for wine with lunch. Drive times run from under 5 to 50 minutes from the house.

Cave B is a short walk from the house, and the rest are a drive through wine country. Many tasting rooms keep reduced winter hours, so check each card before you go. We recommend them because we like them, and we take no commissions. Drive times are from the house.

No. 01

Cave B Estate Winery (Quincy Tasting Room)

$$ 10 min walk walkable tasting with river views

The estate tasting room sits next door to the Gorge Amphitheatre, with a lavender-lined piazza, vine-covered pergola and lawn looking toward the Columbia River. A tasting is six pre-selected wines, normally three whites and three reds, with baguette and brie, cheese, salami and other snacks sold alongside. Winter weekends bring free live music in the downstairs Winter Wonderland cave. Reservations are only for groups of 8 or more.

Winter hours per the winery: Mon, Wed, Thu 12 to 5:30 pm; Fri and Sat 11 am to 6:30 pm; Sun 11 am to 6 pm; Tue closed until late spring. Last tasting starts 45 minutes before closing. Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day; closes 4 pm New Year's Eve.

Leashed, friendly dogs are welcome outside only, per Grant County Health Department rules. Kids of all ages are welcome.

 Pro Tip

Tuesdays are closed until late spring, and the room is closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Call 509-785-3500 ext. 0 before a winter visit.

No. 02

Beaumont Cellars Quincy Tasting Room

$$ 30 min drive quiet rural estate tasting

Where Beaumont began: a rustic tasting room tucked into a working winery and warehouse on the family's 120-acre homestead east of Quincy. The covered Crushpad next door hosts seasonal dinners with local chefs, spring to late fall. The feel is deliberately rural and unhurried, with estate reds like the Cabernet Franc on the list. This is a tasting room and winery, not the Woodinville bistro, and winter hours are weekends only.

Winter hours: Fri, Sat, Sun 12 to 5 pm per the winery's own site; check its events calendar for seasonal hours.

 Pro Tip

Winter is Friday to Sunday only. Check the events calendar for seasonal hours, and note Crushpad dinners need reservations.

No. 03

Jones of Washington Quincy Tasting Room

$$ 30 min drive relaxed tasting, food from market vendors

A family winery whose Jones family has farmed around Quincy since the 1950s, with its tasting room inside the Quincy Public Market. Pours include wine by the glass or bottle, wine slushies and cocktails, and you can order charcuterie boards and food from other market vendors to enjoy at the tasting room. The setting is a bright, modern common space, easy with kids in tow. Reservations are not required.

Mon to Sat 12 to 6 pm, Sun 12 to 5 pm per the winery; occasionally closes early for private events.

 Pro Tip

It sometimes closes early for private events, so call before the drive. Reservations are only requested ahead for groups over eight.

No. 04

Discovery Cider Company (Ephrata)

$$ 45 min drive local cider, early evening stop

A small cider house in downtown Ephrata, moved over from Tacoma, where hard ciders are made on site and poured in the tasting room. Press coverage describes six ciders and three beers on tap plus two wines, so there is something for non-cider drinkers. It is an evening stop, open Thursday to Saturday only and closing at 9 pm. Call before a visit to confirm food and whether kids are welcome, which its own site does not say.

Per its own site: Thu to Sat 4:30 to 9 pm; closed Sun to Wed.

 Pro Tip

Open Thursday to Saturday evenings only; the Sunday hours shown on map apps conflict with its own site, which says closed.

No. 05

Gård Public House (Royal City)

$$ 50 min drive wine with lunch, open daily

The Royal City outpost of Gård Estate Wines, a wine tasting room that doubles as a public house with a food menu and inn rooms upstairs. Gård pours its Columbia Valley estate wines, and the site lists a lunch menu all day on Sundays. It is the most relaxed place in the area for a sit-down lunch with a glass of wine. Call ahead for kids, dogs and tasting fees, which the winery's site does not state.

Per the winery's own site: Sun 11 am to 6 pm; Mon and Tue 11 am to 8 pm; Wed and Thu 11 am to 9 pm; Fri and Sat to 10 pm (site shows a typo on the Fri/Sat opening time).

 Pro Tip

It is open every day including Sunday, so it works as a fallback when the Quincy tasting rooms are closed on a Tuesday.

Questions about wineries near The Gorge

Can I walk to a winery from the house?

Cave B Estate Winery's Quincy tasting room is about a 10-minute walk. Its winter hours are Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 12 to 5:30, Friday and Saturday 11 to 6:30, and Sunday 11 to 6. Tuesday is closed until late spring.

How much is a tasting at Cave B?

Cave B lists a $20 tasting fee, or $25 if you keep the glass. Reservations are only needed for groups of 8 or more. Kids are welcome, and leashed dogs are allowed outside only.

Which tasting rooms are open in winter?

Jones of Washington is open Monday to Saturday 12 to 6 and Sunday 12 to 5. Beaumont Cellars is open Friday to Sunday 12 to 5. Discovery Cider is open Thursday to Saturday evenings only.

Which tasting rooms are open on holidays?

Cave B is closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, with reduced hours on New Year's Eve. Check the others' sites before you drive, since holiday hours are not always posted.
