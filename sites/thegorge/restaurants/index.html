No. 01

Tendrils Restaurant at Sagecliffe

$$$ 15 min walk special dinner with wine

The resort restaurant at Sagecliffe, set among the vineyards a short walk from the house, and the closest sit-down dinner to the Gorge Amphitheatre. The menu leans farm-to-table, with steaks, lamb and seasonal plates, paired with Sagecliffe Cellars wines and a full bar. Order a glass on the terrace if the weather allows, then settle in for the longer dinner. Reservations are strongly encouraged, and the resort has its own parking right at the door.

Call to confirm hours before you go. Third-party listings disagree (brunch daily and dinner roughly 4 to 9 pm on most nights, with some days closed), and the resort's own site could not be loaded for this check.

 Pro Tip

Book through the resort and call the day of; one reviewer arrived with a reservation and found it closed, and winter hours are reduced.

No. 02

Idle Hour Eatery & Spirits

$$ 25 min drive burgers and a drink

The downtown Quincy go-to for a burger and a drink, and the place locals send people for the bourbon bacon burger and the green chili burger. The room is a relaxed bar and grill with a full bar and house cocktails, plus breakfast items such as an omelet and a benedict on its weekend menu. It is on B Street in the old downtown, with street parking out front, so allow a few minutes to find a spot on a busy night.

Call to confirm hours. Toast shows Sunday 9:00 am to 12:45 pm and closed Monday; other listings show an evening window of about 4:30 to 8 pm.

 Pro Tip

Order the green chili burger; plan around the bar crowd after 8 pm if you have kids with you.

No. 03

Time Out Pizza

$$ 30 min drive family pizza night

A family pizza spot in Quincy with a salad bar and a small arcade, which makes it the easy pick for a group with kids. Order a large pizza for the table and add the salad bar; online ordering is available if you would rather pick it up and eat back at the house. Parking is in the lot on F Street. It is a casual counter-and-booth room, so there is no dress code and no reservation needed.

No own website found; its online ordering page (https://order.online/store/-34498367/) shows 11:30 am to 8:40 pm. Call to confirm.

 Pro Tip

Order ahead online for pickup so the pizza is ready when you arrive.

No. 04

Central Market

$ 25 min drive cheap authentic tacos

A long-running Mexican market with a kitchen counter, where locals go for carnitas and birria tacos at low prices. Order at the counter, then browse the aisles for tortillas, pan dulce and Mexican candy to take back to the house. It is a quick, no-fuss lunch rather than a sit-down dinner. Daytime hours end early, so plan lunch or an early dinner. A 2026 listing shows the building for sale, so confirm it is still operating.

No own website found. Third-party listings show about 8 am to 6 pm Monday and 8 am to 7 pm Tuesday through Saturday, with Sunday not listed. Call to confirm; the property was listed for sale in 2026.

 Pro Tip

Go for lunch and get carnitas or birria by the pound for a cheap group meal.

No. 05

Tacos Jalisco

$ 25 min drive quick tacos after travel

A downtown Quincy taqueria that stays open into the evening, handy after a day on the river or a drive in. Order tacos by the piece and add a torta or a burrito if you are hungry. The room is casual and quick, so it suits kids and tired travelers. It sits a block from the old downtown on C Street, with street parking nearby.

No own website found. A directory lists Monday and Tuesday 9 am to 8:30 pm; call to confirm the rest of the week.

 Pro Tip

Come in the early evening; it is a quick stop, then continue to the amphitheatre.

Questions about restaurants near The Gorge

Can I walk to dinner from the house?

Tendrils Restaurant at Sagecliffe is about a 15-minute walk. Third-party listings disagree on its dinner days, so call (509) 787-8000 to confirm hours and make a reservation, especially in winter.

Where can I eat with kids?

Time Out Pizza in Quincy has a salad bar and a small arcade, which makes it an easy family stop. Its online ordering page shows 11:30 am to 8:40 pm. Tacos Jalisco and Central Market are casual and quick.

What is open early or on a Sunday?

Idle Hour Eatery & Spirits lists Sunday hours from 9 am to 12:45 pm on its Toast page and is closed Monday. Hours across these places vary by source, so call to confirm before you drive 25 minutes.

Where can I get cheap tacos?

Central Market, a Mexican market with a kitchen counter, and Tacos Jalisco, a downtown taqueria, both serve tacos at low prices. Both are about 25 minutes from the house.
