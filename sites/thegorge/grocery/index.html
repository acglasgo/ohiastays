Top 5 Grocery Stops
Near The Gorge

Stock the kitchen before you settle into the quiet.

The best grocery stops near The Gorge are Akins Fresh Market and IGA Quincy Market in Quincy for the main stock-up, Jake's Meats for butcher cuts, Sandoval's Market in George for last-minute snacks and ice, and Safeway in Ephrata for a big-brand shop. Drive times run 15 to 45 minutes from the house.

No. 01

Akins Fresh Market

$$ 25 min drive Full arrival-day grocery stock-up

A family-run full-service grocery that has anchored Quincy since 1963, and the biggest one-stop shop closest to the house. Expect a full produce section, butcher counter, bakery, dairy, beer and wine, and ready-to-cook basics, plus a weekly ad with local deals. It opens at 5:30 AM and closes at 11 PM every day, so a late arrival after a concert or a drive in from Seattle still works. Parking is a plain, easy lot on F Street with no line to speak of outside the usual after-work rush.

Open every day, 5:30 AM to 11 PM (store site, checked 2026-11-25).

 Pro Tip

Do the big shop here on arrival day, including ice and water, since nothing near the house sells a full week of groceries.

No. 02

IGA Quincy Market (El Mercado)

$$ 25 min drive Deli, Latin groceries, cookout supplies

A locally owned IGA on First Avenue SW that also goes by El Mercado, with a deli and prepared foods counter alongside full grocery aisles. It is a good second option when you want fresh tortillas, Latin pantry items, fruit and meat for a cookout. Hours run 7 AM to 9 PM daily, a bit earlier to close than Akins. Reviews are mixed, so treat it as a solid backup and a place for specialty items rather than your only stop.

Daily 7 AM to 9 PM (store site, checked 2026-11-25).

 Pro Tip

Check the weekly ad on its site first; if Akins is out of something, this is the quickest second stop in town.

No. 03

Jake's Meats

$$ 20 min drive Butcher cuts, jerky, quick tacos

A working butcher shop, mini market and taco counter on Highway 281 on the way up from George, run by Alicia Canchola for nearly 20 years. Buy fresh jerky, house sausages, marinated carne asada and chorizo for the grill, then eat a plate of birria or asada tacos at the tables inside. The mini market also stocks spices, canned goods and beer. It sits on the road between the house and Quincy, so it fits a stop on the way in.

 Pro Tip

Buy grill meat here, then fill in produce and staples at Akins; hours are listed inconsistently online, so call (509) 787-4374 (listed on Yelp and MapQuest) before a special trip.

No. 04

Safeway (Ephrata)

$$ 45 min drive Pharmacy, brand names, big shop

The nearest big-chain supermarket, in Ephrata, with a full bakery, deli, pharmacy and a fuel station on site. This is the place for brand-name items, dietary specialty products, prescriptions or a large shop when you are coming in from the Ephrata side. It runs 5 AM to 1 AM, the longest hours of any option here. The parking lot is large and flat, with plenty of space for an RV or loaded SUV. A Walmart Supercenter and Grocery Outlet are also in town if you want a cheaper bulk run.

5 AM to 1 AM daily (Safeway store page, checked 2026-11-25; holiday hours may differ).

 Pro Tip

At 45 min from the house this is a planned trip, not a quick errand; combine it with a drive from Seattle via Ephrata, or skip it for Akins unless you need a pharmacy or specific brands.

No. 05

Sandoval's Market (George)

$ 15 min drive Last-minute snacks, ice, drinks

A small neighborhood grocery and convenience store in George on the south frontage road, the closest place to the house to grab milk, eggs, snacks, drinks and ice if you forgot something. Local listings show it open about 8 AM to 8 PM daily. Selection is basic compared with Quincy, and it has no website, so treat hours and stock as approximate. It is the quickest stop between the house and Interstate 90.

 Pro Tip

Use it as the top-up stop for ice, drinks and forgotten items, not for a full stock-up; address and hours vary across listings, so confirm by phone if timing matters.

Questions about groceries near The Gorge

Where is the nearest grocery store?

Sandoval's Market in George is the closest, about 15 minutes away, but it is a small convenience stop and its listed hours conflict. Akins Fresh Market and IGA Quincy Market are 25 minutes away and are the full-size stores.

What are the store hours?

Akins Fresh Market is open every day from 5:30 am to 11 pm, and IGA Quincy Market is open daily from 7 am to 9 pm, per their sites. Safeway in Ephrata lists 5 am to 1 am, and holiday hours may differ.

Where can I buy steaks or meat?

Jake's Meats in Quincy is the butcher, about 20 minutes from the house, with cuts, jerky and quick tacos. Call ahead, since it has no website of its own.

Should I shop before I arrive?

Yes, if you can. The house is rural, with no store within a short walk, so most guests stock up in Quincy or on the way in. The closest full-size stores are 25 minutes away.
