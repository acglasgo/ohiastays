Welcome to The Gorge

We are so glad you are here. This modern two-bedroom home in The Lakes at The Gorge sleeps up to 8, with a private hot tub, a gas fireplace, a coffee bar and long views over the vineyard to the Columbia River.

Cave B Estate Winery is a 2-minute walk away and the Gorge Amphitheatre is minutes away. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. Here is how everything in the home works, with our favorite places to explore after that.

Around the House

The private hot tub is on the patio. The wind is powerful here. It will take the cover if it is not securely clipped, so keep the cover on and clipped whenever the tub is not in use. Sand and dirt blow in otherwise.

Opening the hot tub:

  • Unclip the front (road side) clips by pinching the outer edges, and unclip the clips on the sides.
  • Fold the front of the cover back over the other half.
  • To remove the cover completely, unclip the back clips. Two people can lift it off and set it on the cement patio.
  • Adjust the temperature as you like, and close the lid while it heats.

Closing the hot tub: reverse the steps above. Always latch the clips on the cover between uses. If needed, tug the cover toward you lightly, since the clips are intentionally tight to keep the wind from lifting it.

Use of the hot tub is at your own risk. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. No glass in or near the hot tub, and no soaps or oils in the water. No jumping or diving. Do not enter if the water is over 104F. When the tub is not in use, keep the cover on and clipped.

Tips: we recommend a quick shower with soap and water before you get in, wiping sand off your feet, and entering and exiting carefully so no one slips. Keep soaks to about 20 minutes, and set the temperature to 98F or lower when you get out. To protect the heaters, we suggest changing the temperature no more than twice in 5 minutes. Anyone with heart disease, diabetes, blood pressure concerns, a serious illness or a pregnancy should check with a doctor first.

The gas fireplace works with the remote stored on the green side table in the living room. Please put the remote back there when you leave. Use of the fireplace is at your own risk.

The living room has big windows, a coral sofa and cozy chairs, and the sofa folds out into a bed for extra guests.

To make the sofa bed:

  • Move the coffee table forward.
  • Remove all the pillows, including the back cushions, and set them somewhere off the floor.
  • Roll out the bottom support frame until it stops.
  • Grab the large cushion and unfold it onto the frame.
  • Find the sheets and the blanket in the bedroom closest to the fireplace.

The full kitchen has everything for group meals, with a fully stocked coffee bar (K-cups, teas and creamers). The 84-inch dining table seats the group for post-winery gatherings and pre-concert dinners. There are board games in the cabinet.

An outdoor grill and a propane tank are available for your use. Grill use is at your own risk.

Tip: please leave the grill clean after use so it is ready for the next cook.

There are 2 bedrooms, each with a king bed, a hair dryer and a stand-up mirror. Together with the sofa bed, the home sleeps up to 8.

Bathrooms are stocked with shampoo, conditioner, hand soap and lotion.

The stacked washer and dryer are in the bathroom area off the kitchen. Press the power button and add detergent: pods go inside the drum, and powdered detergent goes in the dispenser.

Cave B Estate Winery is a 2-minute walk for tastings and sunsets, and the Gorge Amphitheatre is minutes away, with concerts all summer. Tendrils Restaurant and the Sagecliffe Spa are available with reservations, but the Sagecliffe pool is not included.
