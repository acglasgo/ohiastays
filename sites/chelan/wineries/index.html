Top 5 Wineries & Breweries
Near Chelan

Tasting rooms, vineyard patios and a downtown brewpub on Lake Chelan.

Chelan, Washington has five wineries and breweries we recommend: Stormy Mountain Brewing, Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro, Lake Chelan Winery, Tsillan Cellars and Karma Vineyards. Stormy Mountain is a 10 minute walk from east downtown, and the four wineries are 5 to 12 minutes away by car, each with food on site.

Lake Chelan wine country begins at the edge of town, with tasting rooms, vineyard patios and a downtown brewpub all within a short trip. These are the five we recommend, from a family barbecue and beer stop to sparkling wine in a vineyard bistro. We take no commission from any of them. Hours were checked on November 23, 2026, and they shift with the season, so confirm before you go.

No. 01

Stormy Mountain Brewing

$$ 10 min walk walkable family BBQ and beer

Chelan's downtown brewpub is a loud, easygoing room of big shared tables and cozy booths, built for families who want a real meal and a cold pint in the same stop. The menu is barbecue first, with full meat platters and street tacos, alongside house beers and a rotating lineup of local guest taps. It sits about half a mile from east downtown, so the walk back replaces the designated-driver conversation.

Daily 11:30 am to 8:00 pm per the venue site.

 Pro Tip

Skip the car: it is an easy downtown walk, and the large semi-private group area is worth asking about if your party is 6 or more.

No. 02

Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro

$$ 5 min drive lake-view lunch with live music

A lakeside winery and farm-to-table bistro on Spader Bay, with an elevated patio view over the water and jet skiers in summer. The kitchen cooks with vegetables and herbs from its on-site gardens, and the menu swings seasonally, including a winter seafood menu. The site bills it as family and dog friendly, with live music on Saturday nights. It is a short drive from downtown and one of the most recommended stops by locals.

Thursday to Sunday 12:00 to 8:00 pm; closed Monday to Wednesday.

 Pro Tip

Aim for the Friday Abode Happy Hour (3 to 7 pm, $5 glass pours that benefit the Chelan Valley Housing Trust), and remember they are closed Monday through Wednesday.

No. 03

Lake Chelan Winery

$$ 11 min drive lawn, BBQ, and kid-friendly tasting

The region's first bonded winery, set on a large grass lawn where kids can run while adults taste from a deep wine list. A fast-casual BBQ counter on site makes it an easy lunch stop; reviewers single out the kielbasa. The tasting room can fill up with a line right at opening, and the BBQ serves lunch only, not dinner.

Sun to Thu 11 am to 6 pm; Fri and Sat 11 am to 7 pm. BBQ is not served for dinner.

 Pro Tip

Arrive mid-afternoon rather than at opening to skip the line, and order the kielbasa from the BBQ counter.

No. 04

Tsillan Cellars

$$$ 9 min drive Italian dinner with lake views

A villa-style estate on the south shore with rolling vineyards, gardens, and a clock tower looking over the lake. The tasting room pours red blends, and Sorrento's Ristorante on the property serves Italian dishes such as crispy calamari and linguine il pescatore, which makes it the easiest full dinner-plus-wine outing on the list. The winery changed hands to a local ownership group in mid-2026 and continues to operate.

Tasting room Mon to Thu 1 to 5 pm, Fri 1 to 7 pm, Sat 12 to 7 pm, Sun 12 to 5 pm; hours vary seasonally, call 509-682-9463.

 Pro Tip

On live-music afternoons Sorrento's takes no reservations before 4:30 pm and seating is first come, first served, so arrive early or go on a quiet weekday.

No. 05

Karma Vineyards

$$ 12 min drive sparkling wine and a French-style lunch

Founded in 2006 as the first Methode Champenoise sparkling producer in the Lake Chelan AVA, Karma pairs bubbles with a French-leaning bistro serving Washington-fresh dishes inside or out. The property includes a roughly 3,000 sq ft underground wine cave, a patio overlooking vineyards and a koi pond, and it is dog friendly. Their tasting room is open year-round, which makes it a reliable winter pick when many wineries scale back.

Thursday to Sunday 11 am to 4 pm; closed Monday to Wednesday.

 Pro Tip

Book online through their reservation link and go before lunch, since the winery and bistro close at 4 pm.

Questions about wineries and breweries near Chelan

Which Chelan wineries and breweries are open year-round?

Stormy Mountain Brewing lists daily hours from 11:30 am to 8 pm. Lake Chelan Winery pours daily, and Karma Vineyards is open year-round Thursday to Sunday, 11 am to 4 pm. Tsillan Cellars hours vary by season, so call 509-682-9463.

Which of these are good with kids?

Stormy Mountain Brewing is a family brewpub with big shared tables and barbecue. Lake Chelan Winery has a large grass lawn for kids, and Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro bills itself as family and dog friendly. Karma Vineyards does not state a kids policy, so call ahead.

Which wineries are closed Monday to Wednesday?

Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro is open Thursday to Sunday only, and Karma Vineyards is open Thursday to Sunday, so both close Monday to Wednesday. Stormy Mountain Brewing and Lake Chelan Winery are both open daily. Tsillan Cellars is open Monday to Thursday afternoons.

Can I walk to any of them from downtown Chelan?

Stormy Mountain Brewing is about a 10 minute walk from east downtown Chelan. The four wineries are 5 to 12 minutes away by car, so plan a designated driver or a ride for them.
