Top 5 Sunset Drinks & Live Music
Near Chelan

Lakefront pubs, vineyard patios and music nights, picked by the hosts of Ohia Stays.

Chelan, Washington has five good places to enjoy a Lake Chelan sunset with a drink and live music: Campbell's Pub & Veranda, Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro, Tsillan Cellars & Sorrento's Ristorante, Rocky Pond Estate Winery (Chelan Tasting Room) and Even Keel Roadhouse. Music nights fall on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, with lawn concerts from April through September.

We recommend these five because each pairs a good glass or pint with a patio view, a lawn or a stage. Hours and music nights were checked on November 23, 2026, but they shift with the season, so confirm before you go. We earn no commission from any of these venues.

No. 01

Campbell's Pub & Veranda

$$ under 5 min drive lakefront drinks with Friday music

The pub and veranda at historic Campbell's Resort sit right on the Lake Chelan waterfront, so a drink here comes with an easy stroll along the shore afterward. The bar program leans on Pacific Northwest beer, local wines and crafted cocktails, with pub-style dinners like the braised short rib. The room is a family-friendly atmosphere with TVs for the game, and reservations are available through the resort's online booking.

Open daily 8 am to 8 pm per the resort's dining page; Pub Live is Fridays 6 to 8 pm. A resort notice has mentioned a short January closure for upkeep (reopening Jan 15), so call 509-682-2561 for early January stays. The Beach Bar and Shaka Shack are summer only.

104 W Woodin Ave, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Pub Live music runs every Friday, 6 to 8 pm. Come around 5:30 pm in winter to get a table before the music starts, and park once for the whole evening since the resort is a short walk from downtown.

No. 02

Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro

$$ 5 min drive vineyard views and Saturday live music

A hilltop winery and farm-to-table bistro above the north shore of Lake Chelan, with vineyard rows running toward the water and a wide view across the lake. Order a glass or a tasting flight and something from the bistro menu, then settle on the patio as the light drops. The winery describes itself as family and dog friendly, and it hosts live music on Saturday nights.

Tasting room and bistro open Thursday to Sunday, 12 to 8 pm; closed Monday to Wednesday.

105 WA-150, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Abode Happy Hour on Fridays, 3 to 7 pm, has $5 glass pours that support local affordable housing, which makes it the easiest sunset-hour stop. Book the bistro online (up to 8 guests) because same-day tables go by phone only.

No. 03

Tsillan Cellars & Sorrento's Ristorante

$$$ 10 min drive special-occasion dinner with a lake view

An Italian-style estate winery on the south shore of Lake Chelan, with a tasting room, estate vineyards and Sorrento's Ristorante, which the winery says earned a 2026 DiRoNA Award of Excellence. Expect white-tablecloth dining with lake views and estate wines by the glass, plus live music on select days listed on the winery's events calendar. It is the dressier choice on this list, so it suits a celebration or a quiet adult dinner.

Sorrento's dinner Thursday to Sunday 5 to 8:30 pm, lunch Saturday and Sunday 12 to 2 pm. The tasting room is closed Thanksgiving Day.

3875 US-97A, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Reserve Sorrento's by phone at 509-682-9463 and ask for a window table when you book. Check the events calendar for Thursday prime rib nights and live music before you pick a day.

No. 04

Rocky Pond Estate Winery (Chelan Tasting Room)

$$ 10 min walk Thursday tacos, wine and live music

A modern downtown tasting room on Woodin Avenue, an easy walk from the lake, serving wine flights alongside small plates from a farm-to-table kitchen. On Thursday evenings it runs Tunes & Tacos with live music, $10 tacos and $10 pours. Seating is limited and the room is a wine-first space, so it works best for adults or older kids who will be happy at the table.

Fall 2026 hours: Tue and Wed 2 to 6 pm, Thu 2 to 7:30 pm, Fri and Sat 12 to 6 pm; closed Sunday and Monday. Hours change by season.

212 E Woodin Ave, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Tunes & Tacos starts at 5:30 pm on Thursdays. Arrive early or make a reservation online, since the tasting room is small and the taco night draws locals.

No. 05

Even Keel Roadhouse

$ 10 min drive summer lawn concerts, relaxed and casual

A relaxed roadhouse with a big outdoor lawn, Adirondack chairs and red umbrellas, serving rotating craft beer and cider taps, local wines and comfort food. From April through September it hosts live music on the lawn in the evenings, so this is the one to choose on a warm summer night. The venue says it is dog friendly and the lawn gives kids room to roam, but check hours before you drive out.

The site lists Thursday and Friday 3 to 9 pm, Saturday 12 to 9 pm and Sunday 12 to 8 pm, but that page is dated 2024. The business has been listed for sale, so check hours before you go (509-888-5326). Lawn music runs 6 to 9 pm, April through September only.

90 Lees Pl, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Beer and cider sampling is complimentary, so ask for a taster before you commit to a pint. In summer, arrive before the 6 pm music start to claim lawn chairs.

Questions about sunset drinks and live music near Chelan

Which Chelan spots have live music on a Friday or Saturday night?

Campbell's Pub & Veranda hosts Pub Live on Fridays from 6 to 8 pm, and Vin du Lac Winery & Bistro hosts live music on Saturday nights. Both are under 10 minutes by car from east downtown Chelan.

Where can I hear live music on a Thursday in Chelan?

Rocky Pond's Chelan tasting room runs Tunes & Tacos on Thursdays from 5:30 pm, with $10 tacos and $10 pours. Tsillan Cellars also lists live music on select days on its events calendar, so check it before you pick a day.

Which of these work for a winter sunset?

Winter sunsets arrive around 4:30 pm. Campbell's Pub & Veranda is open daily until 8 pm and Rocky Pond's tasting room is open Tuesday to Saturday afternoons. At Tsillan Cellars, dinner starts after dark, so go for lunch or a tasting.

Where are the summer lawn concerts?

Even Keel Roadhouse hosts live music on its lawn from 6 to 9 pm, April through September, and says it is dog friendly. The business has been listed for sale, so check hours before you go.

Which of these are family friendly?

Campbell's Pub & Veranda describes a family-friendly room, and Vin du Lac calls itself family and dog friendly. Even Keel's lawn gives kids room to roam. Rocky Pond suits adults or older kids, and Tsillan Cellars is the dressier choice.
