Near Chelan

Where to stock the kitchen, from butcher-counter steaks to a full one-stop run.

The best grocery stops near Chelan, WA are Bear Foods Natural Market & Cafe for organic and local finds, Safeway for a one-stop run, Chelan Market Fresh for butcher-counter steaks and brats, Walmart Supercenter for cheap bulk stock-ups, and Chelan Ranch Organics Fruit Stand for organic apples and berries any hour.

Chelan has a good mix of grocery options, from a full supermarket to an independent butcher counter and a natural foods store. Most are within a few minutes of downtown, so you can split your list across stops and still be back quickly. No. 01

Bear Foods Natural Market & Cafe

$$$ About 10 min walk organic, local, gluten-free finds

A cozy, wood-shelved natural foods store on Woodin Avenue, with a chainsaw-carved bear greeting you at the door. Shelves of bulk grains, nuts and dried fruit sit beside organic produce, local beef and pork, gluten-free staples, and a good Washington wine and beer wall. A small creperie and juice bar is attached. It is a short, flat stroll from downtown, and parking is easy on the street out front. Hours are Mon-Sat 9 am-5 pm and Sun 9:30 am-5 pm.

 Pro Tip

Fill a bag from the bulk bins for trail mix before a hike or boat day, and grab local meat here for the grill. Staples are cheaper at Safeway, so use this stop for the special items.

No. 02

Safeway

$$ About 15 min walk one-stop big grocery run

The full-service supermarket in town, set on the Manson Road side of downtown with the Chelan hills behind it. It has a deli with made-to-order sandwiches, an in-store bakery, produce, meat and seafood, beer, wine and liquor, a pharmacy, and a fuel station on N Columbia St. The lot is large, which helps when you are loading a week of groceries for a big group. Shelves thin out on big holiday weekends. The store is open 6 am-10 pm daily.

 Pro Tip

Use DriveUp & Go pickup or delivery to have the arrival-day grocery order ready as you pull into town. In July and August shop before 10 am or after 7 pm to skip the lake-day crowds.

No. 03

Chelan Market Fresh

$$ About 17 min walk butcher-counter steaks and brats

A smaller, old-fashioned independent grocery (listed on some sites as IGA Chelan Fresh) just west of downtown, known for its long fresh-meat case with house-made brats, USDA Choice cuts and ready-to-grill marinated options. The produce is strong, there is a good run of Washington wines, and the deli makes burger baskets and, on weekends, a taco plate. Aisles are shorter than Safeway's, so lines and parking are usually easier.

 Pro Tip

Pick up the steaks, brats and local apples here, then finish the rest of the list at Safeway. The deli gets backed up at lunch in summer, so order ahead by phone at (509) 682-4521.

No. 04

Walmart Supercenter

$ 5 min drive (1.2 mi) cheapest bulk stock-up run

The budget stock-up option on the east edge of town at the base of the hills, with a full grocery department, bakery, deli, pharmacy, garden center and general merchandise under one roof. Come here for bulk items, diapers, snacks for the kids, camping and RV supplies, and anything you forgot to pack. The lot is big and flat, and the grocery entrance is on the left side as you face the building. Hours are listed as 6 am-11 pm daily.

 Pro Tip

Best value for water, drinks, paper goods and winter essentials such as ice melt and extra layers. Skip the deli here and buy the fresh meat and produce at Chelan Market Fresh or Safeway.

No. 05

Chelan Ranch Organics Fruit Stand

$$ 10 min drive (5.2 mi) organic apples and frozen berries, any hour

A certified-organic family fruit ranch up the hill north of town, with a rustic self-serve fruit stand and fridge-freezer by the lavender rows, looking out over the lake. It is stocked with organic apples (Honeycrisp, Fuji, Gala, Cosmic Crisp and more) and frozen organic berries, and the stand takes cash, check or Venmo. U-pick and the farm store are closed for the season in winter, but the self-serve stand stays open. It is a scenic stop for fruit and snacks, not a full grocery. The self-serve stand is open 24/7.

 Pro Tip

Bring cash or use Venmo for the self-serve fridge, and grab a bag of organic apples for the car or a bag of frozen berries for smoothies. Call (509) 881-7137 if you want something specific, since apple varieties sell out.

Questions about grocery stops near Chelan

Which grocery store in Chelan is best for a big group?

Safeway is the one-stop pick, with a deli, bakery, produce, meat and seafood, beer and wine, and a large lot. It is open 6 am-10 pm daily. Walmart Supercenter is the cheaper choice for bulk items, drinks and paper goods.

Where can I buy local meat and produce in Chelan?

Chelan Market Fresh has a long fresh-meat case with house-made brats and USDA Choice steaks, plus strong produce. Bear Foods Natural Market carries local beef and pork and organic produce. Both are close to downtown.

Are Chelan grocery stores open in winter?

Yes. Bear Foods, Safeway and Walmart list the same hours year-round, and none has a seasonal closure. Chelan Market Fresh hours come from a third-party listing, so call ahead before a late run. The Chelan Ranch fruit stand is self-serve and open 24/7.

Is there a grocery store walkable from downtown Chelan?

Bear Foods Natural Market on Woodin Avenue is about a 10 minute walk, Safeway is about 15 minutes, and Chelan Market Fresh is about 17 minutes. Walmart Supercenter and Chelan Ranch Organics are a short drive away.
