Around the House

The hot tub is open October 1 to April 30 and the pool is open May 1 to September 30. Both are in the backyard with a waterfall and a fire feature, and two sliding doors lead straight out from the lower level.

There is no lifeguard, so use of the hot tub and pool is at your own risk. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. Please keep glass out of and away from the hot tub and pool, and keep soaps and oils out of the water.

Hot tub: no jumping or diving, and please do not get in if the water is over 104F.

Tips: we recommend a quick shower with soap and water before you get in, keeping the cover on whenever the tub is not in use, and entering and exiting carefully so no one slips. We suggest keeping soaks to about 20 minutes. Anyone with heart disease, diabetes, blood pressure concerns, a serious illness or a pregnancy should check with a doctor first.

The fire feature by the water runs on propane. The propane tank is on the left side of the backyard water area.

To turn it on:

  • Remove the protective box lid.
  • At the top of the tank, turn the silver knob to the "open" position.
  • Move the green lever on the gas hose switch downward.
  • Use the lighter provided, holding it at the furthest edge of the fire feature.

To turn it off:

  • Turn the silver knob to the "close" position.
  • Lift the green lever back up to where you found it.
  • Cover the propane tank with the protective box lid.

Please use the fire feature and the fireplaces safely, and turn the fire feature off after use.

Safety: we recommend taking care around the propane tank and keeping the area clear of anything flammable.

Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

The main-level living room is a cozy spot with a smart TV, comfortable seating, an indoor fireplace and french doors that let in plenty of light. There is also a queen pull-out couch here for extra sleeping space.

The living-room fireplace runs on propane. Turn it on with the wall timer on the left side of the fireplace. The timer helps avoid leaving it on for long stretches by accident.

The lower level is set up for movie nights, with a 100-inch projector screen, sound system, a sectional couch, two bean bag chairs, and plenty of pillows and blankets.

The Samsung projector and entertainment system are yours to use. The white remote controls power, sound and all features. You can even talk into it!

The converted backyard shed is a home gym and workout space, a handy way to start the day before heading out to the lake.

The full kitchen has a dishwasher, induction stove, microwave and a pantry of small appliances, ready for big group meals. For coffee lovers there is a coffee pot, Keurig and an espresso machine.

Next to the kitchen is the waffle bar, with a waffle maker, syrups, sprinkles and other treats. There is also a wine fridge, a wet bar and a set of assorted bar glasses.

An outdoor grill is ready for backyard feasts, along with a dining table and chairs for meals outside. In high season, sun loungers and outdoor games are out for your use.

Tips: we recommend leaving the grill clean after use so it is ready for the next cook. On bad-weather days, the lounger cushions are happiest in the shed.

The backyard lights are on the house, the shed and a string of lights. The switches for the shed and string lights are at the left corner of the shed.

Please turn the backyard lights off by 11 PM.

The washer and dryer are in the laundry room off the kitchen. Press the power button and add detergent: pods or sheets go inside the drum, and powdered detergent goes in the dispenser.

A baby gate and a pack and play are provided. The loft has carpeted floor space that is perfect for playtime.

Main level: the primary bedroom has a queen bed, a claw-foot bathtub, a walk-in closet and a flat-screen TV. There is a half bathroom on this level, and a queen pull-out couch in the living room for extra sleeping space.

Loft: a bedroom with two twin beds and one full bed, two closets with hanging racks and drawers under the twin beds, and a full bathroom.

Lower level: a queen bed with a chest of drawers and hanging rack, and a full bathroom with a walk-in shower.

Bathrooms are stocked with shampoo, conditioner, body wash, hair dryers and robes for a spa-like touch.

Out front you will find two sitting chairs and a swinging bench for your morning coffee, a hummingbird feeder and a birdfeeder to enjoy nature up close, and mountain views.
