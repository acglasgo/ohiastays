Top 5 Family Fun
in Chelan

A summer waterpark, a lakefront playground, winter snow tubing and easy indoor days.

Our top five family fun picks in Chelan, WA are Slidewaters Waterpark, Don Morse Memorial Park, Echo Valley Ski & Tubing Area, Chelan Lanes and Chelan Riverwalk Park. They cover a summer waterpark, a lakefront playground and beach, winter snow tubing, year-round indoor bowling and a gentle stroller-friendly riverside loop.

We recommend these five because each one works for kids, with options for every season. Slidewaters is summer-only (Memorial Day to Labor Day) and Echo Valley is winter-only, so check the season before you plan your day.

No. 01

Slidewaters Waterpark

Summer only $$$ 5 min drive summer splash days with kids

Chelan's hillside waterpark has a lazy river, tube and body slides and a kids' splash area, all set against the dry golden hills above the lake. Families settle onto the lawn chairs by the river and rotate between slides and the snack grill. Lakeside Surf, a separate stationary surf-wave attraction, shares the grounds with its own hours and tickets. This is a summer-only stop: the park runs Memorial Day to Labor Day and is closed the rest of the year.

Season: Open Memorial Day to Labor Day only; check slidewaters.com for daily hours.

 Pro Tip

Arrive near opening and claim lawn chairs by the lazy river before the midday crowd. The park is in full sun, so pack sunscreen and a cover-up for toddlers.

No. 02

Don Morse Memorial Park

Free 22 min walk beach, playground, and putting

This 40-acre lakefront park is the town's all-purpose family playground, with a modern play structure beside a sandy beach and 2,000 feet of waterfront. Beyond the swings there are a skatepark, pickleball and tennis courts, picnic shelters and The Green, an 18-hole grass putting course. In summer the park adds go-karts and paddleboard and kayak rentals; in winter it turns into a quiet lakeside stroll. Parking in the lot is paid.

Hours and season: Park open daily 7 am to 11 pm; most activities run Memorial Day to Labor Day weekend.

 Pro Tip

Park once and let the kids burn energy at the playground, then walk the lakeshore path to the marina. No dogs from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day.

No. 03

Echo Valley Ski & Tubing Area

Winter only $$ 26 min drive snow tubing with young kids

A small, volunteer-run community hill in the pines above Manson, and the easy winter day for families with little ones. The six-lane tubing hill takes kids 3 and up and the area supplies the tubes, so there is nothing to bring but warm layers. A simple lodge handles warm-up breaks, and there is a beginner ski lift. This is a winter-only stop, open daily around the holidays and then weekends and holidays only until Presidents weekend, weather permitting.

Season: Tentative opening Dec 19; daily to Jan 3, then weekends and holidays; lifts and tubing from 10 am.

 Pro Tip

Check the website schedule the morning you go, because the season depends on snow and opening day is only tentatively December 19. Bring tire chains or traction tires for the road up.

No. 04

Chelan Lanes

$$ 23 min walk rainy-day or winter indoor fun

Chelan's local bowling alley is the dependable rainy-day and snowy-evening option, on the main road into town. It has bumpers for small bowlers, an arcade and pool tables, and an on-site restaurant with food and drink menus. Weekend nights often add cosmic or black-light bowling. The alley is first come, first served with no reservations, so walk in and expect a short wait on holiday weekends. It is open year-round.

Hours: They vary by month and are posted on the website as an image, so call 509-682-2251 to confirm.

 Pro Tip

Go earlier in the day or early evening for the family crowd and ask for bumpers for young kids. Bring socks, which the alley asks for.

No. 05

Chelan Riverwalk Park

Free 13 min walk easy stroller-friendly riverside walk

A 12-acre Chelan PUD park along the short Chelan River, with a paved, stroller-friendly loop of about a mile and wide grass lawns for running around. There is no playground, so think of it as a gentle outing for families with strollers or little walkers, with picnic areas, restrooms and views of the river and bridge. It sits close to downtown, and is free and open all year, dawn to dusk.

Hours and season: Day use year-round, 7 am to dusk (Chelan PUD).

 Pro Tip

Walk here from downtown and skip parking, which can be tight. The loop is flat and paved, so it works for strollers and scooters all winter.

Questions about family fun in Chelan

Is Slidewaters open in winter?

No. Slidewaters Waterpark is summer-only, running Memorial Day to Labor Day, and it is closed the rest of the year. For a winter family day in Chelan, we recommend Echo Valley snow tubing, Chelan Lanes bowling or a stroll at Riverwalk Park.

When does Echo Valley open for snow tubing?

Echo Valley Ski & Tubing Area is winter-only, with a tentative opening of December 19, daily through January 3, then weekends and holidays until Presidents weekend, weather permitting. Check its website schedule the morning you go.

What can families do in Chelan on a rainy day?

Chelan Lanes is the easy indoor pick, with bumpers for small bowlers, an arcade, pool tables and an on-site restaurant. It is open year-round, but hours vary by month, so call 509-682-2251 before you head over.

Which Chelan family spots are free?

Don Morse Memorial Park and Chelan Riverwalk Park are both free to enter. Don Morse has a playground, beach and skatepark, though parking in its lot is paid. Riverwalk has a paved stroller loop and lawns but no playground.
