Five places to eat that we send our own friends to.

The best restaurants in Chelan, Washington are Local Myth Pizza, County Line Eat & Drink on Woodin, Campbell's Pub & Veranda, Marcela's Cocina Mexicana and Sorrento's Ristorante at Tsillan Cellars. Four are within a 15 minute walk of east downtown Chelan, and Sorrento's is a 10 minute drive out Highway 97A.

These are the five places we send friends and family when they ask where to eat in Chelan, covering easy family pizza, a walkable pub dinner, a big downtown dining room, a margarita-and-burrito night and one special-occasion Tuscan dinner. No. 01

Local Myth Pizza

$$ 11 min walk easy family pizza night

A casual downtown pizzeria where hand-rolled, hand-crafted artisan pizzas and calzones come out alongside fresh salads, antipasto and homemade desserts. The tap list runs ten local beers deep with more than thirty local wines, so parents and the rest of the table are covered. Dine in, take out, or grab a take-and-bake pie to cook at home; online ordering is available if you want it ready when you walk in.

Open Tuesday through Sunday from 11:30 am; closed Mondays.

 Pro Tip

Order a take-and-bake pizza and salad on arrival night and cook it in your own kitchen so nobody has to get back in the car after a long drive.

No. 02

County Line Eat & Drink on Woodin

$$ 8 min walk casual walkable dinner with drinks

A relaxed, family-welcoming spot on East Woodin Avenue serving scratch-made food and cocktails, with local wines and Pacific Northwest beers and ciders. The vibe suits a post-swim summer lunch or a winter evening after a day of skiing or snowshoeing. Dogs are welcome on the patio, and a separate Nomad Food Truck menu is posted on the restaurant's site.

Open Thursday through Monday, 12 to 9 pm; closed Tuesday and Wednesday.

 Pro Tip

It is an easy walk from east downtown and one of the closest good dinners, but the kitchen is closed Tuesday and Wednesday, so plan those nights elsewhere.

No. 03

Campbell's Pub & Veranda

$$ 15 min walk all-day family dining downtown

The long-running dining room at Campbell's Resort, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner daily with Northwest-inspired dishes, seasonal ingredients and a full bar. Guests regularly mention the chop chop salad, fire pasta, fish and chips and blackened Caesar, and the resort describes it as a family-friendly restaurant. Several TVs make it an easy pick on game nights, and tables can be booked through the resort's online reservation link.

Serves breakfast, lunch and dinner daily; call 509-682-2561 to confirm current winter service times.

 Pro Tip

Book ahead for summer evenings and holiday weekends; it is also the town's go-to for a big holiday buffet, which fills early.

No. 04

Marcela's Cocina Mexicana

$$ 13 min walk group Mexican dinner downtown

A downtown Mexican restaurant built on family recipes, fresh ingredients and homemade tortillas. The wet burritos are the standout, especially with chile verde pork in a tomatillo sauce, and the bar pours roughly 30 margarita flavors for the adults. Walk-ins are the norm and online ordering runs through Toast, which is handy for taking dinner home.

Listings disagree on hours, so call 509-682-4754 before you go.

 Pro Tip

Order the chile verde wet burrito and check the restaurant's Facebook page for the day's specials.

No. 05

Sorrento's Ristorante at Tsillan Cellars

$$$ 10 min drive special-occasion Italian dinner with views

A Tuscan-style dinner house on the Tsillan Cellars estate, serving cucina povera built on local meat and produce. The glass-enclosed pavilion with an Italian fireplace keeps it comfortable year round, with Lake Chelan views in summer and snow-capped mountains in winter. The restaurant calls it a warm family dining experience, and it hosts seasonal specials such as a Thanksgiving buffet and December prime rib Thursdays. Reserve by phone or OpenTable.

Dinner Thursday to Sunday 5 to 8:30 pm; lunch Saturday and Sunday 12 to 2 pm. Reservations: 509-682-9463.

 Pro Tip

Book a table at sunset in summer or by the fireplace in winter, and note it is dinner Thursday to Sunday only, so reserve ahead for those nights.

Questions about restaurants near Chelan

Which Chelan restaurant is best for an easy family pizza night?

Local Myth Pizza downtown is our pick. It serves hand-rolled pizzas, calzones and salads, pours ten local beers and more than thirty local wines, and sells take-and-bake pies for eating at home. It is open Tuesday through Sunday.

Which of these restaurants need a reservation or have limited days?

Sorrento's serves dinner Thursday to Sunday, so reserve at 509-682-9463 or on OpenTable. County Line's kitchen is closed Tuesday and Wednesday, Local Myth is closed Mondays, and Campbell's is worth booking ahead for summer evenings and holiday weekends.

Can I walk to dinner in downtown Chelan?

Yes, four of the five are walkable from east downtown: County Line is 8 minutes, Local Myth 11, Marcela's 13 and Campbell's 15. Sorrento's at Tsillan Cellars is a 10 minute drive out Highway 97A, so plan a ride or a designated driver.

Where should we go for a special-occasion dinner in Chelan?

Sorrento's Ristorante at Tsillan Cellars. The glass pavilion has an Italian fireplace and views of the lake in summer or snow-capped mountains in winter. It is the priciest of the five at $$$ and takes reservations.
