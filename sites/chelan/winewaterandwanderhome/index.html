Welcome to Chelan

We are so glad you are here. This in-town Chelan house has 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms for up to 8 guests, with a game room, a rec room and a year-round fire pit. In summer there is a private pool too. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Downtown Chelan and the lake are about a 10-minute walk away, and the valley's wineries are a short drive. Here is how everything in the house works, with our favorite places to explore after that.

Essential Info

4 PM check-in. This home has self check-in, so you can arrive on your own schedule after 4 PM.

Your door code and address arrive in your check-in message, 3 days before check-in.

Hoping to arrive earlier? Message us through your booking app and we will do our best.

Free parking is right next to the house, across from the car wash. Use the small driveway or park parallel to the gate. There is also free street parking nearby, and gated parking is accessible from the car wash side.

A car is the easiest way to reach the wineries and explore the lake. Downtown Chelan and the lake are about a 10-minute walk, so you can leave the car parked for dinner and beach time.

WiFi details are in your check-in message.

The thermostat is on the main floor, across from the staircase. Turn the display ring to set the temperature.

To switch between Heat and Cool, press the ring in for the menu, turn to Settings and press, turn to Heat or Cool, then press again to confirm.

Tip: we recommend keeping the A/C around 68F or warmer and the heat around 74F or cooler, with doors and windows closed. It keeps the house comfortable and efficient.

Quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM.

The house is on a busy main road, next door to a car wash that can be noisy during its operating hours, so some street noise is expected. Light sleepers may want to bring ear plugs.

10 AM check-out. Before you head out:

  • Trash goes into the on-site bins, which are on the side of the house.
  • Lights and TVs off.
  • Lock any doors and windows you opened during your stay.

Around the House

The pool is open about Memorial Day to Labor Day and is closed the rest of the year. There is no lifeguard on duty, so swim at your own risk. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. No glass in the pool area, and keep the pool gate closed.

Tips: we recommend a quick shower with soap and water before you get in, and skipping soaps and oils in the water. It keeps the pool clean for everyone.

The fire pit area has comfortable seating and is open year-round. Never leave a fire unattended, put it out completely before going inside, and follow any local burn bans.

An outdoor BBQ grill and utensils are ready for you, along with a dining table and chairs for meals outside, and sun loungers for sunny afternoons.

Tips: we recommend leaving the grill clean after use so it is ready for the next cook.

The living-room fireplace (electric glow) and the metal wood-look stove are decorative only, for ambiance. They are not for fires or heat. Use the thermostat to warm up the house.

The full kitchen has a stove and oven, refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, toaster, blender, rice maker, baking sheet and cooking basics. You will also find a coffee maker, coffee, a hot water kettle, wine glasses, dishes and silverware.

The sun-filled dining nook seats up to 5 guests.

Handy for movie nights: a fridge, Keurig, air fryer, popcorn maker, sink and dishwasher, right where the fun is.

Head downstairs for ping pong, a big-screen TV and lounge seating in the game room. The wellness corner has an exercise bike, yoga mats and hand weights, handy for a quick workout before lake day.

A private, botanical speakeasy with moody floral wallpaper, velvet curtains and a round game table with seating for four. It is a cozy spot for cards, board games or unwinding after a day on the lake.

The washer and dryer are in the basement rec room. Press the power button and add detergent: pods or sheets go inside the drum, and powdered detergent goes in the dispenser.

A crib, pack 'n play and high chair are provided, along with children's books and toys, children's dinnerware and outlet covers. The playroom has soft floor seating, books and puzzles.

Bedroom 1 has a king bed and room-darkening shades. Bedroom 2 has a queen bed. The living room and the movie room each have a queen sofa bed for extra sleeping space.

Both bathrooms have walk-in showers, hot water, shampoo, conditioner, body soap and a hair dryer. Fresh towels, extra pillows and blankets are provided.

The main gathering space has a smart TV, comfortable seating, board games and large windows with a view toward the hills. It sits right beside the kitchen.

There is a desk in the bedroom shown here, and the table in the sunny room with sheer curtains makes a bright second spot to work if you need to catch up on email between lake days.
