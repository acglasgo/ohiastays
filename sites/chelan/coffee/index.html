Top 5 Coffee Shops
in Chelan

Espresso stands, a bakery, a coffee and wine bar and all-day breakfast.

Our top five coffee shops in Chelan, WA are Apple Cup Too, The Vogue Coffee Bar, Lake Chelan Artisan Bakery, The Family Bean and Goldie's Coffee Shop. They cover a quick espresso stand, a downtown coffee and wine bar, a from-scratch bakery, an all-day breakfast spot and a light-filled late-breakfast cafe.

We recommend these five for different kinds of mornings, from a 5am weekday espresso on the way to the lake to a slow acai bowl with a lake-side backyard. Several pour Blue Star coffee, roasted in Twisp. Hours shift in winter, so confirm before you go.

No. 01

Apple Cup Too

$ Under 5 min walk fastest morning coffee run

A tiny wood-trimmed coffee stand on East Woodin Avenue, the sister to the Apple Cup Cafe diner, and one of the closest espresso stops to downtown. It pours Blue Star coffee, roasted in Twisp, and opens at 5am on weekdays, so it suits early risers. It is a small stand, not a sit-down cafe: order, then take your cup to the lake or back to the fire pit.

Hours: Mon-Fri 5am-6pm, Sat-Sun 6am-6pm; confirm in winter.

Address: 804 E Woodin Ave, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Walk over in the morning and keep going to the waterfront, since you are already headed that way. The stand is a separate business from the Apple Cup Cafe diner at 114 E Woodin, whose hours differ.

No. 02

The Vogue Coffee Bar

$$ 9 min walk downtown latte and wine bar

Downtown Chelan's all-day coffee bar on East Woodin Avenue, billed by its owners as the valley's gathering place. It pours Blue Star coffee alongside breakfast and lunch plates, fruit smoothies, pastries and homemade treats. The full bar adds seasonal cocktails, local Chelan wines and draft beer, so it works for a morning latte or a late-afternoon glass. The room is relaxed and chatty, and you can order ahead online through Toast.

Hours: Mon-Sat 7am-4pm, Sun 7am-2pm.

Address: 117 E Woodin Ave, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Place your order on the Toast link from their website before you walk over on summer mornings, then skip the counter line. Come back after 11am or on a winter afternoon to try the local Chelan wines and draft beer from the bar.

No. 03

Lake Chelan Artisan Bakery

$ 19 min walk pastries, bread and espresso

A from-scratch bakery in the Chelan Plaza strip on West Manson Highway, next to the Safeway. It serves Blue Star espresso with handmade French and Italian pastries, bagels, cookies and pies. Savory choices include breakfast burritos on house-made tortillas, quiche, soups and sandwiches on house-baked bread. It is a convenient stop when you are already shopping for groceries, and the loaves and bags of Blue Star beans make good take-home gear.

Hours: the shop does not list them, so call (509) 423-3717 before a Sunday or holiday visit.

Address: 246 W Manson Hwy, Suites 3 & 4, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Buy a loaf of bread and a bag of Blue Star beans on your way out; both are sold to take home, which makes it a good stop before a breakfast back at your place. The venue site lists no hours, so call (509) 423-3717 before a Sunday or holiday visit.

No. 04

The Family Bean

$ Under 5 min drive all-day breakfast with kids

A family-run, community-minded coffee shop a few blocks from downtown that roasts its coffee in-house. Breakfast and lunch run all day while supplies last: breakfast sandwiches, burritos, biscuits and gravy, a sourdough Reuben, French dip and turkey melt, plus smoothies and acai bowls. Every drink comes with a cinnamon-sugar mini donut on the lid, an easy win with kids. It is counter service and closed Sundays.

Hours: Mon-Fri 7am-3pm, Sat 8am-2pm, closed Sunday.

Address: 131 S Apple Blossom Dr #101, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Come before the lunch rush for a breakfast sandwich, since food is served only while supplies last, and ask about the fresh mini donuts. Plan Sundays elsewhere because it is closed.

No. 05

Goldie's Coffee Shop

$$ 10 min walk late breakfast, acai bowls and lattes

A bright, plant-filled shop on East Woodin Avenue that pairs specialty coffee with acai bowls, smoothies and matcha. Lattes, a honey cinnamon latte and mushroom-boosted chagaccinos sit beside bowls topped with homemade gluten-free granola, and many items are vegan or gluten-free. Tall arched windows fill the room with light, and there is backyard seating by the lake. It is a short walk from downtown and open daily from 9am to 2pm, so it suits a slow late breakfast.

Hours: Open daily 9am-2pm; confirm holiday hours in winter.

Address: 106 E Woodin Ave, Chelan, WA 98816

 Pro Tip

Use the Order Online button on goldieschelan.com to skip the line, then take your cup toward the waterfront. The shop closes at 2pm, so plan your visit before lunchtime. The menu is labeled for gluten-free and vegan choices if anyone in your group needs them.

Questions about coffee shops in Chelan

Which Chelan coffee shop is closest to downtown?

Apple Cup Too is the nearest, about a 3 minute walk from east downtown Chelan, with The Vogue Coffee Bar at 9 minutes and Goldie's Coffee Shop at 10 minutes on foot. All three sit along East Woodin Avenue, so you can walk to coffee and keep going to the waterfront.

Which Chelan coffee shop opens earliest?

Apple Cup Too opens at 5am Monday to Friday and 6am on weekends. The Vogue Coffee Bar and The Family Bean both open at 7am on weekdays. Goldie's Coffee Shop opens at 9am daily. Confirm holiday and winter hours with each shop before you go.

Where can I get breakfast with kids in Chelan?

We recommend The Family Bean for all-day breakfast while supplies last, including breakfast sandwiches, burritos and biscuits and gravy. Every drink comes with a cinnamon-sugar mini donut on the lid. It is counter service, and it is closed on Sundays.

Which Chelan coffee shops are open on Sunday?

Apple Cup Too is open Sundays from 6am to 6pm, The Vogue Coffee Bar from 7am to 2pm and Goldie's Coffee Shop from 9am to 2pm. The Family Bean is closed Sundays. Lake Chelan Artisan Bakery lists no hours online, so call (509) 423-3717 first.

Are there vegan or gluten-free coffee options in Chelan?

Goldie's Coffee Shop labels its menu for gluten-free and vegan choices, with acai bowls topped with homemade gluten-free granola, smoothies, matcha and specialty lattes. Order online through its website to skip the line, and note that it closes at 2pm every day.
